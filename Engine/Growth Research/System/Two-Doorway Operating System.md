# The Two-Doorway Operating System
### A repeatable engine for "build structure, systems & support" · v1 · June 2026

> One prospect list. Two doorways. Five plays. Every step scripted so it runs the same way every time — and so a VA or an agent can run it without you.

---

## The spine (how every business moves through the system)

```
SOURCE ──► SCORE (≥7.5 rubric) ──► FREE VALUE (no strings) ──► PROBLEM CAPTURE
   │                                                                │
   └──────────────► CRM (every record, every touch) ◄──────────────┘
                                  │
              ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
        DOORWAY 1: SELL GROWTH                  DOORWAY 2: PARTNER / GROW / ACQUIRE
        1a  % of every lead (high ticket)        2a  Free value → digital partner
        1b  Free website → review → referral     2b  Broker the deal (funding + finders)
        1c  Intro to a buyer/broker → finder fee
```

**Rule of the system:** nobody is "sold to" cold. Everyone gets value first (a website, a visibility fix, an honest problem diagnosis). The ask is always a *review and the truth about their problem*. Revenue is a by-product of being useful at scale.

---

## SOURCE → SCORE (the filter that protects your time)
- Source only businesses that fit the gap profile: **sensible margin + weak digital** (no site, or GBP-only with stale/no reviews and no auto-review engine).
- Score every one on the **/10 rubric** (digital gap 3 · margin 2.5 · reachability 1.5 · deal fit 2 · location 1).
- **≥7.5 = work it. 5–7.4 = honourable mention (nurture/automate). <5 = drop.** No exceptions — this is what stops you emailing people who'll never buy.

## FREE VALUE → PROBLEM CAPTURE (the universal opener — your `1b` made standard)
The same opening works for *both* doorways, which is why it's the spine, not a branch:
1. Lead with a **no-strings free build** — a one-page website or a visible fix (Google Profile sorted, reviews turned on). "Built this for you. Yours to keep. No catch."
2. The only ask: **"If it's useful, leave us a review."** That's the whole transaction if they don't want more.
3. Before you leave, ask the **problem-discovery question**: *"What's the one thing in the business that, if it were fixed, would make the biggest difference this year?"* Even if you can't fix it — **log it**. That data is the asset.
4. Everything (the business, the free asset delivered, the review, the stated problem) goes into the **CRM** with a next action.

# DOORWAY 1 — SELL GROWTH

### 1a · Percentage of every lead (high-ticket only)
**What:** For businesses where one lead is worth a lot (dental implants, legal cases, surveys, aesthetic procedures), don't sell a website — sell **leads on rev-share**. We build + market at our cost/risk and take **a % of every qualified lead or booked job we generate**.
- **Structure:** define a "qualified lead" in writing (form fill / booked call / attended consult). Track via call-tracking number + form attribution so there is no dispute. Take **10–20% of the booked-job value**, or a flat **£/£€ per qualified lead** agreed up front.
- **Best when:** lead value > ~£500/€600, owner has capacity to take more work, score deal_fit = 2.
- **Why it wins:** owner risks nothing, you're aligned to outcomes, and it compounds into a retainer.
- **Guardrail:** attribution must be airtight before you start. No tracking = no deal.

### 1b · Free website → review → referral (the volume play)
**What:** The universal opener *as the whole offer* for local/lower-ticket businesses. Give the website free, ask for a review, ask **"who else do you know who needs this?"**
- **Structure:** free one-pager (productised, AI-foundation + light human polish so it costs you almost nothing). Upsell path: hosting/care plan, reviews engine, local SEO retainer — but only if they ask.
- **Best when:** local sweet-spot sectors, deal_fit = 1.5, low ticket.
- **Why it wins:** referrals from a delighted owner are your cheapest, warmest pipeline. The free build is marketing spend, not charity.

### 1c · Intro to a buyer/broker → finder's fee
**What:** When a sourced owner actually wants to **sell** (not grow), you don't walk away — you **introduce them** to a broker or buyer on your network list and **collect a finder's fee**, without exposing full details up front.
- **Structure:** signed one-line **introducer agreement** first (protects your fee). Send the buyer/broker a **blind teaser** (sector, region, rough turnover, "weak digital, owner retiring") — no name/contact until they sign. Typical introducer/finder fee: **2–10% of the deal** or a flat fee per completed introduction; confirm each broker's referral terms in the CRM.
- **Best when:** owner's real intent is exit, not growth.
- **Why it wins:** monetises the "wrong fit for growth" prospects you'd otherwise bin.

# DOORWAY 2 — PARTNER / GROW / ACQUIRE

### 2a · Free value → digital growth partner
**What:** For emotionally-attached owners who don't want to sell: give value free, prove it, then propose **"keep your business — we grow it"** and share the upside. Your sales manager (human or AI) delivers the value digitally first.
- **Structure (the three from One-Pager 2):**
  - **Rev-share growth partner:** you build + run the digital engine, take **10–15% of incremental revenue** above an agreed baseline for ~3 years. Owner keeps 100%.
  - **Earn-in:** modest retainer + **earn up to ~30% equity over 3 years** against milestones (EMI options), optional call-option to buy the rest at ~4× EBITDA.
  - **Funded build:** government grant covers ~50% of the build (see 2b), halving the owner's cash risk and making "yes" easy.
- **Best when:** attached owner, asset-backed, real cash flow, score margin = 2.5.

### 2b · Broker the deal (funding + finder network)
**What:** Two revenue lines from your network: (i) **funding** — use the grants/loans below to fund a client's growth or a deal, taking a fee or % for sourcing it; (ii) **finder** — connect deals to specialist brokers/buyers for a cut, and learn each broker's **specialty** so you route deals correctly.
- **The funding stack (the "70%" assembled — confirm live windows before pitching):**
  - **Ireland:** LEO **Trading Online Voucher** (~50% up to €2,500 — *perfect for funding a client's first website*), Enterprise Ireland, LEO grants.
  - **UK/Scotland:** Growth Guarantee Scheme (70% loan guarantee, to £2m), Made Smarter (50% up to £20k, manufacturers), UKSPF growth grants (~50%), Help to Grow: Management (90%), Business Gateway DigitalBoost (Scotland).
  - *(The fleet is pulling the exact current schemes, caps and eligibility per region — slotting in when it returns.)*
- **Sourcing criteria (find the deals + the funding ourselves to broker):** profitable (margin signals), weak digital, owner 55+/exit-curious, asset-backed, in a grant-eligible region/sector. Match each to a scheme + a broker/buyer.

## The Objection-Handling Vault
*(Owners are typically 45+, sceptical, time-poor, and hate being "sold to." Calm, specific, no hype. These go straight into the interactive PDF.)*

| # | Objection | The line | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"I already get all my work by word of mouth."** | "That's exactly why this works — you've earned the reputation, but 87% of people still Google you before they call. Right now they find nothing, or a profile with no reviews, and they ring the next name. We just make sure the reputation you already have shows up." | Validates them, reframes the gap as *protecting* existing equity, not chasing new. |
| 2 | **"I don't have time for this."** | "You don't need any. We build it, we run it, you carry on with the job. The only thing we need is ten minutes to hear what's actually slowing the business down." | Removes the effort objection entirely; the ask shrinks to a conversation. |
| 3 | **"Websites are a waste of money / I had one, did nothing."** | "Agreed — a brochure site does nothing. We don't sell pages, we sell booked jobs. If it doesn't bring you work, you don't keep paying. And the first one's free." | Separates you from the bad past experience; moves to outcomes + risk reversal. |
| 4 | **"How much is this going to cost me?"** (1a) | "Nothing up front. We build it and market it at our cost. We only get paid a slice of the leads we actually bring you — if you don't get work, we don't get paid." | Risk fully on you; aligns incentives; disarms price resistance. |
| 5 | **"What's the catch with a free website?"** | "No catch. It's yours. All we ask is an honest review if it's any good, and that you tell us the one thing holding the business back — even if we can't fix it, it's useful for us to know." | Honesty is the differentiator; the 'no strings' is real and they feel it. |
| 6 | **"I'm thinking of selling / retiring soon."** | "Then let's talk about that too. A tidy online presence lifts what a buyer will pay — and if you're serious about selling, I know the right brokers and buyers and can make a quiet introduction." | Converts a 'no' into doorway 1c/2 — the finder/partner play. |
| 7 | **"AI / my nephew can do a website for free now."** | "They can do the page. They can't get you found, answer the calls you miss, chase the reviews, and follow up the leads. That's the bit that actually makes money — and it's what we run for you." | Reframes against commoditised builds; sells the system, not the artefact. |
| 8 | **"I don't trust handing my business to an outsider."** | "Fair. That's why we start by giving, not taking — a free build, no contract, no obligation. You decide if we've earned the next conversation." | Trust is bought with the free value; the structure proves it. |

## Compliance & guardrails (do this right — "skin the cow accurately")
- **UK B2B outreach (PECR/GDPR):** cold *email/SMS* to **corporate bodies** (Ltd, LLP) is permitted with a clear opt-out; **sole traders & partnerships are treated like individuals** — needs more care, prefer phone or a soft-permission step. Cold *calling* is fine if the number isn't on the **TPS/CTPS** — screen against it.
- **Ireland:** similar GDPR + ePrivacy regime — corporate email B2B with opt-out; screen and keep it relevant.
- **Finder's fees / introductions:** always a **signed introducer agreement before** you reveal the business; never share identifying details pre-signature.
- **Deal-broking / funding (2b):** promoting investment can trigger **FCA financial-promotion rules** — keep arrangements as commercial service contracts; **legal sign-off before any investor-style pitch**. Confirm grant windows with the local Growth Hub / LEO before relying on funding in a pitch.
- **Data:** CRM holds public business contact data for a legitimate-interest B2B purpose; log source + date per record; honour opt-outs immediately.

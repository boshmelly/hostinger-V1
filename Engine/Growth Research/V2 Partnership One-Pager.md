# Grow With Us — Keep Your Business, Share the Upside
### A partnership proposal for established owners · Precision

> *For the owner who's built something real over years or decades — and isn't looking to hand it over, just to make it work harder.* A client-facing one-pager. Swap `[Business]`/`[Owner]` per prospect; keep it to one page.

---

**[Owner]**, you've spent years building **[Business]** into a name people trust. The work is excellent. The reputation is earned. The one gap is **visibility and systems** — the business is hard to find online, the enquiries that do come in aren't captured or followed up, and the growth that's sitting right there never gets switched on.

We don't want to buy that out from under you. **We want to grow it with you — and only get paid when it works.**

## Three ways we can work together

**1 · Grow-and-share (you keep 100%).**
We build and run the digital engine — website, "get found" visibility, lead capture, missed-call answering, reviews, follow-up. You keep full ownership. We take a **share of the *extra* revenue we create** (typically 10–15% of the growth above today's baseline, for ~3 years). If we don't grow it, we don't get paid.

**2 · Growth partner (earn-in).**
For owners who want a long-term partner: a small running fee plus we **earn a minority stake over 3 years — only by hitting agreed targets.** Skin in the game, aligned to your numbers.

**3 · Step back on your terms.**
If what you actually want is to slow down or eventually exit, we'll structure that too — keep the name, keep your people, and we either grow it for a planned handover or introduce the right buyer. Your legacy, protected.

## Why this is low-risk for you
- **You don't pay up front.** The models above pay us out of growth, not your pocket.
- **Government funding can cover much of the build.** Depending on where you are: **Ireland's Trading Online Voucher (~50%, up to €2,500)**, **Made Smarter (~50%, up to £20k)**, the **Growth Guarantee Scheme**, or local growth grants — we handle the paperwork. *(Subject to eligibility — we confirm before anything starts.)*
- **You stay in control.** Nothing changes about how you run the business unless you want it to.

## Who you're working with
Run by **Ola Seweje** — nine years coordinating contractors on Transport for London infrastructure programmes (tens of millions in works), APM/PMP qualified. Calm, organised, delivery-first — not a tech salesperson. *Proof of method: a heating & cooling firm in Essex went from ~2–3 jobs a week to ~6–8 after we set up missed-call capture and follow-up — about £3,200/month they were previously losing.*

## What happens next
A 20-minute conversation. We look at where **[Business]** is leaking enquiries today, agree what "growth" actually means in your numbers, and pick the model that fits. **No cost, no obligation, and we'll tell you honestly if we're not the right fit.**

> **[Owner]** — one question to start: *if one thing about the business were running better a year from now, what would you want it to be?*

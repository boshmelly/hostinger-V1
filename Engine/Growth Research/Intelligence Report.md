# Gap-Intelligence Report — Pilot Run
### Weak-digital, sensible-margin SMEs · Ireland · Scotland · outer London · 28 June 2026

> **What this is:** a precision pilot — not a 1,000-row scrape. 67 candidates were discovered by 18 region×sector agents, 42 were taken to a verification + scoring pass, and **every one in this report was confirmed to be a real, currently-trading business** against a public source (Companies House/CRO, GBP, Yell/GoldenPages). Each is scored /10 on the qualification rubric; **≥7.5 qualifies**, 5.0–7.4 is an honourable mention.

## Headline
- **36 qualified** businesses (≥7.5) and **6 honourable mentions**, all verified.
- Spread: **Ireland 18 · Scotland 17 · outer London 7**.
- Confidence: **27 High · 15 Med** (Med = some contact/digital detail still to confirm before outreach).
- The pattern that repeats: **established, reputation-rich, often single/family-owned businesses with NO website at all — only stale directory/Google listings and no review engine.** Dental labs, funeral directors, opticians, private dental, chartered surveyors and specialist retail dominate the top of the list.
- Deal-model fit across the set — 1b free site: 23 · 1a lead %: 19.

## The repeatable method (so this runs the same way every time)
1. **Source** by region×sector for the gap profile (sensible margin + weak digital).
2. **Verify** each business exists via a public source — drop anything unconfirmed.
3. **Score /10**: digital_gap (3) · margin (2.5) · reachability (1.5) · deal_fit (2) · location (1).
4. **Tier**: ≥7.5 qualified · 5.0–7.4 honourable · <5 dropped.
5. **Enrich**: best doorway, deal model, opening approach, 3 questions, free-value hook, problem-discovery question.
6. **Load** into the CRM with sources attached for pre-outreach verification.

## How to read the deal tags
- **1a lead %** — high-ticket (dental, surveyors, clinics): build + market at our cost, take a % of every qualified lead/booked job. Zero up-front to them.
- **1b free site** — give the website free, ask for a review + referral; upsell only if they ask.
- **2a partner** — attached, asset-backed owners: free value → growth partnership (rev-share / earn-in).
- **1c intro** — owner actually wants to exit → introduce to a broker/buyer for a finder's fee.

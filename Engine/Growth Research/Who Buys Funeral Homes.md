Crucially they entered the island via **Sandy Close Funeral Services (Belfast, Sept 2025)** — their first NI deal since 2019, explicitly to "pave the way for further growth in the region." They are the **only consolidator with stated island-of-Ireland appetite**. Their model is *exactly* the pitch a 3rd-gen owner wants to hear: **name retained, owner stays on, continuity for staff and families.** *(Confidence: High.)*

**2. Larger regional Irish independents.** There is **no proven Republic-of-Ireland roll-up buyer** — Irish funeral homes overwhelmingly change hands by **family succession or a quiet local sale**, not corporate M&A. So your second route is a bigger regional independent expanding locally (the Dublin majors — Fanagans, Massey Bros, Cunninghams — are the visible large independents; a nearer-Offaly operator may be the real local buyer). *(Confidence: Med-High — it's an informal, below-the-radar market.)*

**3. The trade channel: IAFD (iafd.ie).** The Irish Association of Funeral Directors is a *relationship* channel, not a buyer — but it's how you'd quietly find who's acquiring in the midlands.

## How the deal works (so you sound informed)
- **Valuation:** funeral businesses trade at roughly **4–6.5× EBITDA** (incl. freehold), or **~2–3× SDE** for a small single-site operator. The driver is **annual funeral/call volume + trend**; a 100+ year name with a freehold premises carries real **goodwill value**.
- **Structure:** name kept above the door, **owner stays on** (months–years) for continuity, often an **earn-out** tied to maintained volume. Sandy Close is the template.
- **Your finder's fee:** business introductions typically pay **~5%+ of deal value** (range 5–35%); for an off-market intro, a flat or **1–5% success fee, "no sale, no fee."** ⚠️ **Get a one-page introducer agreement signed BEFORE you name the business** — it protects your fee for 12–24 months.

## How to approach Colm Dooley (don't scare him)
- **Lead with legacy, not money.** For a 3rd-gen owner the fears rank: ① the family name dying ② staff let go ③ standards/dignity slipping — **price is often #3 or #4.**
- **Frame it as options, not a sale:** "sell and step back" · "**partner** — keep the name, get back-office/cover support, stop working every weekend" · "**keep and grow**." The Sandy Close story (name stays, family stays, but they can switch off evenings) is your exact script.
- **Be the trusted intermediary, not a broker circling the business.** "I know who treats family firms well and protects the name — no obligation, I'll just lay out what's possible." **Confidentiality first** — one wrong word in a small town spooks staff and grieving families.
- **Remember the live signal:** his pub (The Midland Bar) is already on the market via BidX1 — he's *already* in a winding-down frame of mind. That's why this conversation is timely.

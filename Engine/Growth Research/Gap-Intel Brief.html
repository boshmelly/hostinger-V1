The opportunity

Across Ireland, Scotland and outer London there is a repeatable pattern: established, reputation-rich businesses — dental labs, funeral directors, opticians, private clinics, surveyors, specialist retailers — that make a sensible margin but are nearly invisible online. No website, or just a stale Google listing with a handful of old reviews and no way to collect more. They’re not short of skill or customers; they’re short of visibility and systems. That gap is the whole business.

Two doorways, five plays

1 · Sell growth

1a% of every lead. High-ticket: we build & market at our cost, take a slice of every qualified lead. Zero up-front to them.
1bFree website → review → referral. Give it free, ask for a review and a referral. Upsell only if they ask.
1cIntro → finder’s fee. If they’d rather sell, we introduce a broker/buyer and take a fee.

2 · Partner / grow / acquire

2aFree value → growth partner. Attached owners keep the business; we grow it for a share of the upside (rev-share / earn-in).
2bBroker the deal. Funding stack covers ~50–70% of the build; we source funding & deals for a fee.
★The opener is always the same: give value first, ask for a review and the truth about their problem. No strings.

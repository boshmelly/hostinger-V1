# One-Pager 1 — The Market & Where to Fish
### Kladius / RevenueDrive · Positioning, target client, and geography · June 2026

> **The question you asked:** Where is the agency positioned, *which* owners actually buy websites + growth (so I stop emailing people who'll never buy), and — since the US spends most and the UK is home — what's the profitable **middle ground**?

---

## 1. Geography — go GCC, anchor on Ireland, don't chase the US cold
Your instinct is half right. US clients pay more **on retainers** (~15–20% above UK), but US *website build* prices are near parity with the UK once converted — and the US gives you the worst saturation plus a 5–8h timezone gap. The better play is a **middle-ground market with high budgets and low friction**:

- **🥇 UAE / Dubai (primary).** High SME budgets, English business norms, a construction + government-digitisation boom, and **GMT+4 = full daytime overlap with the UK** (it's *ahead* of you, not behind). SME digital spend growing ~24% CAGR. Best spend-to-friction ratio. *Caveat: sales are relationship-led — expect a local intro/partner or a few trips.*
- **🥈 Ireland (secondary).** Lowest friction of any market: EUR, English, same timezone, UK-familiar contracts. Budgets near UK (not above) — a **delivery-easy** play, not a premium one.
- **🥉 Saudi/Qatar** (biggest GCC pool, more Arabic/localisation), then **Australia** (higher rates but +9–11h timezone kills live delivery).

**Move:** Keep the UK as your proving ground, open Dubai for premium budgets, use Ireland for easy EUR wins.

## 2. Sell *growth*, not a website — AI has commoditised the build
AI builders (Lovable, v0, Framer, Wix AI) now cover the £100/mo basic-site need; 76% of designers name AI as their top threat. The margin has moved to **outcomes and retainers**. Productise: AI-foundation + 20–40h human work = 80% of a custom build at a fraction of the cost, then **monthly outcome retainers** (leads/booked jobs, local + AI-search visibility, reviews engine, reporting). **Cross-sell RevenueDrive AI call-answering as the proof of growth — "we get you the lead *and* make sure you answer it."** That's the bit AI builders can't touch.

## 3. Who actually buys (highest-intent niches — fish here)
| Niche | Why they buy *now* | Reach them via |
|---|---|---|
| **Trades** (electricians, plumbers, roofers, M&E) | 87% of trade searches start on Google; sick of Checkatrade/MyBuilder lead fees; £3k–£50k jobs | Gas Safe/NICEIC registers, trade FB/WhatsApp groups |
| **Independent estate & letting agents** | Revolt against Rightmove/Zoopla fee hikes; want to own their pipeline | Propertymark, Estate Agent Today, local LinkedIn |
| **Independent / used-car dealers** | Auto Trader raised fees 8%; buyers research + finance online | Car Dealer Mag, IMDA, dealer FB groups |
| **Manufacturing / engineering SMEs (B2B)** | 68% of buyers start on Google; 57% of SMEs switching suppliers digitally; only ~37% feel digitally mature | Made in Group, MakeUK, LinkedIn (works in B2B) |
| **Small accountancy practices** | MTD for ITSA from Apr 2026 forces repositioning | ICAEW/ACCA/AAT directories, AccountingWeb |

**Buying triggers to filter on:** portal/directory fee hikes (strongest single trigger) · going after bigger/commercial contracts · succession or sale prep (owner 58–65, no successor) · regulation (MTD) · a new competitor outranking them · recent hiring.

## 4. Who to STOP chasing (protect your time)
Sub-£250k sole traders / one-man bands (haggle to £0) · indie cafés/pubs/restaurants *for websites* (discovery is Google Maps/Insta/Deliveroo) · Shopify/Etsy/Amazon e-com (already served) · "I built it on Wix myself" · anyone asking "how much for a cheap website" — they're buying **cost**, not growth.

---

**Bottom line:** Sell *managed growth + AI answering*, not pages. Target owner-led £500k–£10m firms in trades, property, auto, manufacturing and accountancy who are already paying — and resenting — a platform fee. Win in the UK, price-up in **Dubai**, take easy EUR wins in **Ireland**. Ignore the US until inbound pulls you there.

# One-Pager 2 — The Business-for-Sale Play
### A second doorway: sell growth to retiring owners — or partner / acquire & grow them · June 2026

> **The question you asked:** A lot of people are selling their businesses. Can we get a report on which industries have the most sellers, sell them growth (websites + visibility), or — better — *partner* instead of letting them sell, using government funding so they risk little and we share the upside?

---

## 1. The wave is real — and it's biggest where digital is weakest
- **~1.3–1.5m UK businesses** are owned by people near retirement; **52% of employer firms** are run by owners 55+.
- **~30% of SME owners plan to sell within 2 years** (up from 20% a year earlier); retirement drives **~69%** of SME sales.
- **~65% have no succession plan**; ~80% who try to sell fail to. **620,000 directors are already working past pension age.**
- **~38,000–60,000 UK businesses are openly listed for sale** at any time (Rightbiz ~23.5k, BusinessesForSale ~14k, Daltons ~13k).

**The killer overlap:** the highest-volume *selling* sectors are the same ones with the **weakest digital presence** (~27% of UK SMEs still have no website; in these sectors it's 35–56%). Asset-rich, digitally invisible, emotionally attached, exiting. That's the opening.

## 2. Listings by sector — and the sweet spot
| Sector | For-sale volume | Asking price | Digital maturity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Food & catering** (cafés, takeaways, pubs) | ~9,000+ — the single largest category | £30k–£250k | **Weak** (35–42% no site) |
| **Hair, beauty & barbering** | ~52k businesses, 80% under 5 staff | £15k–£80k | **Weak** (38–44% no site) |
| **Care** (small homes, domiciliary) | £27bn market; 38% of sales now sub-20-bed | £500k–£5m+ | Weak–medium |
| **Trades / construction** | Largest population (15.8% of all firms); mostly sold *off-market* | Asset/goodwill | **Weakest** (45–56% no site) |
| Retail / convenience | ~1.8k listed; ~50k stores exist | £40k–£300k | Weak |

**Sweet-spot targets** (high seller volume + long-tenure attached owners + weak digital + real cash/assets):
1. **Hospitality (cafés/takeaways/pubs)** — biggest pipeline, low ticket → *sell websites + growth* at volume.
2. **Hair & beauty** — micro, cheap to enter → *roll-up / partner* play (digital booking lifts revenue fast).
3. **Small care homes** — high asset value, ageing single operators → *acquire-and-grow* (premium).
4. **Trades** — biggest raw pipeline but sold informally → reach by **direct outreach**, not listing sites.

## 3. The bigger play — partner or acquire, don't just sell a website
For owners emotionally attached to what they built, lead with **"keep your business — we grow it"**, not "sell up."

| Model | How it works | Typical terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Rev-share growth partner** | You build + run the digital engine, take a % of *incremental* revenue. Owner keeps 100%. | 10–15% of uplift, 3 yrs |
| **Earn-in** | Modest retainer + earn equity against milestones (EMI options) | up to 30% over 3 yrs |
| **Buy with growth-funded deferred** | Acquire 51% with little cash down, paid from the growth you create | see template 3 |

## 4. The "70% government" angle — it's real, but you *stack* it
No single scheme hands over 70% as cash. You assemble it:
| Scheme | Funds | Match / cap |
|---|---|---|
| **Made Smarter Adoption** | Digital/AI/automation (manufacturers) | **up to 50%**, cap £20k |
| **Growth Guarantee Scheme** | Acquisition/growth loans | **gov guarantees up to 70%**, to £2m |
| **Help to Grow: Management** | 12-wk growth course + mentoring | **90% funded** (owner pays £750) |
| **UKSPF growth grants** | Digital/productivity (council-run) | ~50%, ~£2.5k–£10k — *windows close Mar 2026* |

*(Note: Help to Grow **Digital** closed in 2023 — don't pitch it. Builds on your existing Start Up Loans 7.5% + BridgeAI £25–50k research.)*

## 5. Three deals you can put on the table
1. **"Keep your business — we grow it":** Made Smarter/UKSPF grant covers ~50% of the build → you take **10–15% of incremental revenue for 3 years** → owner keeps 100% ownership. *Best opener for the attached retiree.*
2. **"Earn-in growth partner":** small retainer + **earn up to 30% equity over 3 years** against EBITDA milestones, with a call option to buy the rest at **4× EBITDA** in year 3.
3. **"Buy 51%, growth-funded":** ~30% via **Growth Guarantee loan** (70% gov-guaranteed) + 40% **seller note** over 3–4 yrs + 30% **earn-out** tied to the growth you create. Near-zero cash down; owner stays on, paid from the upside.

---

**Bottom line:** Two doorways into the same wave. **Doorway A** — sell websites + growth to the thousands of digitally-invisible café, salon and trade owners heading for the door (volume, low ticket). **Doorway B** — for the ones who *don't* want to fully let go, offer a **grant-funded growth partnership or near-cash-free acquisition** and share the upside. Same prospect list, two offers.

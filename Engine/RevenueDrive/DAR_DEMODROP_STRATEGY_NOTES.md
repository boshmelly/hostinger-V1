## The Core Strategy: "DemoDrop"

Dar calls it the **"Digital Donut"** — you deliver a personalized AI demo TO a specific business before they ever talk to you. The demo sells itself. No pitching needed.

### How It Works

1. **Build a demo agent** for a specific business type (e.g., an electrician, builder, contractor)
2. **Use GHL's Voice AI Widget** — embedded inline on a webpage so visitors can literally talk to it or type their business in
3. **Post on social media** (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) showing the demo working
4. **Call to action**: "Comment JELLY (or any word) and I'll DM you the link to try it for YOUR business"
5. **DM everyone who comments** — they get a personalised demo link
6. **Lead captured**: They try it, you have their email/phone from GHL widget → follow up

### Why It Works (Dar's Data)
- Josh closed **15 out of 20 leads** using this approach
- Personalized demos close **3–10x better** than pitching
- AI handles missed calls + after-hours → businesses SEE the value immediately

## Ola's Version of This Strategy

### The Build (What We're Making in GHL)
- **Demo agent**: Electrician/trades business AI receptionist
- **Voice AI Widget**: Embedded inline on a RevenueDrive landing page
- **Visitor experience**: "Type your business name and talk to an AI that answers like your receptionist"
- **Lead capture**: GHL captures name, email, phone when they interact
- **Create GHL opportunity**: Tagged as "AI Demo Lead" → Ola follows up

### The Social Content
- **TikTok / YouTube**: "I built an AI receptionist for a trades business in GHL — watch it live"
- **CTA**: "Comment JELLY if you want the link to test it for your business"
- **DM automation**: GHL workflow sends the link to everyone who comments (via ManyChat or GHL social trigger)
- **Outcome**: Email + phone captured before Ola speaks to a single person

### Why Builder/Contractor is the Right Demo Template
- Ola has existing trades/electrician websites he's built audits for
- Credibility: £38M TfL infrastructure background = they trust him
- These businesses ALWAYS miss calls = they understand the pain immediately
- £110/month is affordable for a sole trader (no budget objection)

# Phase 1 A/B Outreach Sprint

**Sprint goal:** Launch a clean A/B first-touch outreach test (2 wordings, same approach) across our built-site prospects, measured by reply rate.

## KEY FINDING (29 Jun) — changes the channel
Addressable email segment = **only 2** (deployed + has email + not yet emailed).
- 75 sites deployed, but most have **no email captured**; 22 already `outreach_sent`.
- **Email A/B is not viable at scale right now.** We lack the email addresses.
- Therefore **Phase 1 A/B runs on the PHYSICAL MAILER + QR channel** (we have postal addresses), and the QR capture page is what **harvests the missing emails/phones** to feed future email/text.

## The 2 A/B variants (drafted by marketing manager — reusable as mailer headline/copy)
- **Variant A — gift-led / curiosity:** "We made you something" → see your free site.
- **Variant B — benefit-led / proof:** "More {{town}} customers" → see your free site.
- Winner metric: reply/scan rate. Split 50/50. Hold until ~30-40 responses.

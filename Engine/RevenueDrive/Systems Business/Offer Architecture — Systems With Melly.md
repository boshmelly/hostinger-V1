## Ladder 1 — Trades & Local Service Businesses (Electricians First)

The pain: these businesses are busy. They are doing the work. But leads are falling through — missed calls, slow responses, no follow-up system. Every missed call is a missed job. For a trade business billing £500–£3,000 per job, that adds up faster than most owners realise.

---

### Tier 1 — Lead Flow Foundation
**Price:** £200
**Timeline:** 72 hours
**Who it is for:** A trades business that wants to stop losing easy leads without changing how they work day-to-day.

**What they get (GHL deliverables):**
- GHL sub-account set up in their business name
- Lead capture form embedded on their existing site or a simple hosted landing page
- Booking rules configured (available hours, service area, job types they take)
- Call and DM qualification script (what to say, what to ask, when to book)
- Handover checklist: exactly what needs updating each morning to keep it working

**Definition of done:** A customer can contact the business in one step and every incoming enquiry has a clear next action — booked, followed up, or disqualified.

**Content angle:** "I set up a GHL sub-account for an electrician in 72 hours. He got 3 enquiries in the first week from people who had already been on his site for 6 months. The form was the only thing that changed."

---

### Tier 2 — Never Miss a Lead
**Price:** £450
**Timeline:** 5 days
**Who it is for:** A trades team already getting regular calls and messages but losing work because no one picks up after hours, on weekends, or when they are on a job.

**What they get (GHL deliverables):**
- GHL AI receptionist — voice bot or chat widget — trained on their services, pricing range, and availability rules
- Missed-call text-back automation: within 2 minutes, the prospect gets a personalised message
- Follow-up sequence: 3-touch automated sequence (SMS + email) for leads who enquire but do not book
- Booking and lead qualification flow: only qualified leads reach the calendar
- Pipeline view: owner can see at a glance what is open, what is booked, what needs chasing

**Definition of done:** Every missed call gets a response. Every lead is tagged as qualified, unqualified, or booked. The owner no longer has to remember who to chase — GHL does it.

**The gym analogy:** This is like having a PT who tracks your reps when you're not looking and sends you a reminder when you miss a session. You just show up. The system does the admin.

**Content angle:** "The missed-call text-back alone has recovered jobs for electricians who never knew they were losing them. Your phone rings at 6pm Friday. You're finishing a job. You miss it. GHL sends a text in 2 minutes. Monday morning that person is booked."

---

### Tier 3 — Scale Without Chaos (CRM Build)
**Price:** £900–£2,500
**Timeline:** 2–3 weeks (sprint-based)
**Who it is for:** A trades business that is consistently busy, wants to add vans, or is managing multiple job types and needs full visibility across the pipeline.

**What they get (GHL deliverables):**
- Full GHL CRM: pipeline stages mapped to their exact sales and delivery process (enquiry → quoted → accepted → in progress → invoiced → reviewed)
- Quoting workflow: template quotes triggered from pipeline stage, with automated follow-up if not accepted
- Follow-up automation: 5-touch sequence for quotes not accepted within 3 days
- Reviews and referrals workflow: post-job trigger to request Google/Trustpilot review + referral ask
- Reporting dashboard: weekly summary of enquiries, quotes sent, jobs won, conversion rate
- Team training session: 1 hour walkthrough with whoever owns the pipeline day-to-day

**Price variation explained:**
- £900: single trade type, 1 service area, straightforward pipeline
- £1,500: multiple job types, 2 service areas, custom quoting logic
- £2,500: full team setup (3+ people), complex pipeline, integrations (accounting, scheduling)

**Definition of done:** The owner can see every lead, every quote, and the next action — in one view, at any time. Nothing is in someone's head or a notebook.

**The food analogy:** Tier 3 is the difference between cooking from memory every night and having a proper recipe system. Same ingredients. But now it's consistent, scalable, and you can hand it to someone else without them ruining it.

### Tier 4 — Command & Scale (Premium)
**Price:** £5,000
**Timeline:** 4–6 weeks
**Who it is for:** An established trades business with 40 vans, 20–40 staff, a maintenance crew, and multiple locations already running. They have separate departments — accounting, sales, admin — and payroll to manage. They are not asking whether they need a system. They are asking who can build one that holds up at their scale without creating more admin than it removes.

**What they get (GHL deliverables):**
- Multi-location GHL setup: separate pipelines per location or division, unified reporting at HQ level
- Advanced automation stack: full customer lifecycle from first enquiry to repeat booking, review, and referral
- AI receptionist across all locations: consistent responses, consistent qualification, consistent booking
- Team onboarding and training programme: documented SOPs for using GHL, recorded walkthroughs, role-based access
- Quoting, invoicing, and follow-up unified: all job types, all team members, all tracked
- Monthly review cadence (for 3 months post-launch): Ola reviews pipeline health, conversion rates, and automation performance
- Growth infrastructure audit: identify the next constraint before it becomes the bottleneck

**Who pays this:** The director of a trades business running 40 vans who needs the whole operation visible from one dashboard — not because they are overwhelmed, but because they are growing fast and one blind spot at this scale costs more than £5,000. The owner who has tried to "sort out the systems" twice and had it fall apart when a key person left. The operator with 20–40 staff, multiple locations, and a maintenance crew who needs everything talking to everything.

**The argument for £5,000:** One additional van running 5 jobs a week at £800 average job value is £4,000 per week in new revenue. The Command & Scale package pays for itself in the first week of a new van operating. The business owner already knows this — they just need someone who has been in it with them to build it properly.

**Content angle:** "You already know you need the system. The question is whether you build it now while you still have time, or after the wheels fall off."

## Ladder 2 — Claude Setup (Creators / Professionals / DevOps)

The pain: people want to use Claude properly — for their specific workflows, not just random prompts. Most are either not starting, starting wrong, or starting and getting stuck. The £200 entry point removes the barrier. The Workflow Build Sprint is where the real transformation sits.

---

### Tier 1 — Claude Ready in 72 Hours
**Price:** £200
**Timeline:** 72 hours
**Who it is for:** Anyone who wants Claude set up and useful for their actual work — not a generic chatbot, but a configured workspace that knows their requirements.

**What they get:**
- Requirements intake: a short but structured session to capture their before/after, workflows, and priorities
- Claude workspace configured: profile, tone, style rules, and context set up correctly
- 3 starter workflows built and documented: specific to their work (content drafting, client comms, data processing, SOPs — whatever they actually do)
- Handover video: a short walkthrough so they can run it without help on day one

**Definition of done:** The client can run all 3 workflows end-to-end without support. **Success metric: tasks completed without manual effort.**

**The DevOps angle:** Claude Code is what DevOps always wanted. Repos, IaC, pipelines, Kubernetes — the mechanical layer. "We were always the middle ground between dev and ops. Claude removes the mechanical part and leaves the thinking."

---

### Tier 2 — Workflow Build Sprint
**Price:** £750–£1,500
**Timeline:** 7–10 days
**Who it is for:** Operators and creators who want 1–2 automations built properly — requirements, build, edge-case handling, documentation, and a handover that actually sticks.

**What they get:**
- Full workflow design: inputs, outputs, edge cases, fallback logic all mapped before a line is built
- Build delivery: by vetted Claude-specialist implementers, QA'd by Ola before handover
- Documentation: written SOPs for running, maintaining, and updating the workflow
- Lightweight training: 1–2 hours with whoever owns it day-to-day

**Definition of done:** The workflow runs reliably. Inputs and outputs are clear. The client or their team can update it without calling anyone.

## Ladder 3 — Airbnb / Short-Let Direct Booking

The pain: most Airbnb hosts are competing on price on the channel. They do not know the sub-niches, they are not locally visible, and they have no system for repeat or direct bookings. The opportunity is significant for anyone who wants to move off the channel dependency.

---

### Entry — Airbnb Listing & Ops Audit (PDF Checker)
**Price:** £49–£149
**Timeline:** 72 hours
**Who it is for:** Hosts with at least 1 live listing who want to know exactly what is costing them bookings.

**What they get:**
- Scored checklist across: listing quality, photos, pricing, availability settings, operations, local visibility
- Priority list: the top 3 things to fix in the next 7 days
- Sub-niche assessment: which guest types their property is actually suited for (contractors, corporate, SEND support workers, etc.)

**Definition of done:** The host has a ranked to-do list they can act on immediately.

---

### Mid — Direct Booking Systems Course
**Price:** £500
**Format:** Self-paced course (hosted on course.iq)
**Who it is for:** Hosts with at least 1 live listing who want to reduce channel dependency and build direct bookings with repeat guests.

**What they cover:**
- Local search presence: how to appear on page 1 when a contractor or nurse searches for a flat in your area
- Guest sub-niches: holidaymakers are one niche. Contractors, support workers, corporate clients, specialist engineers at sea — these are the others. Each needs something different.
- Direct booking mechanics: how to take bookings without Airbnb, legally and practically
- Ops that protect ratings: cleaning QA, maintenance cadence, check-in friction removal
- Repeat stay strategy: the weekly routine that turns a one-time guest into a returning one

**Real proof from Ola's own hosting:**
- Teachers: 4-month stays
- Support workers managing SEND children: 9-month stays
- Aircraft mechanics: 2-month stays
- Student nurses and corporate clients: regular repeat bookings

**Definition of done:** The host has a repeatable weekly routine that builds local visibility, trust, and direct or repeat enquiries.

## Fulfilment & Quality Rules (Across All Ladders)

1. Ola owns every client discovery call. No implementer speaks to a client before Ola has qualified them.
2. Every build goes through a QA checklist before handover. The client does not see it until it passes.
3. All trades deliverables are built on GHL. No exceptions.
4. The 30% implementer cut applies only to build delivery — discovery, QA, and client comms stay with Ola.
5. Tier 1 (£200) deliverables are designed to be built within 3 days. If they take longer, the model breaks.
6. The "definition of done" for each tier is non-negotiable. If it is not met, the job is not complete.

## Section 1: The UK Scale Player — Who Is Already Doing This?

### Primary Candidate: Moneypenny

The company the founder is thinking of is almost certainly **Moneypenny**, headquartered in Wrexham, Wales. Moneypenny describes itself as the world's leading answering service and the clear market leader in the UK. Its scale is significant: the company handles **over 20 million calls and live chats per year** for approximately **21,000 businesses**, employs over **1,000 staff** across the UK and US, and reported annual revenues of approximately **$369 million** (sourced via Owler/ZoomInfo company profiles, 2025–2026 data). It is backed by UK private equity firm ECI Partners since 2018 and has delivered double-digit revenue growth consistently.

Moneypenny has launched an AI receptionist product — its **AI Voice Agent** — sitting on top of its traditional live-agent telephone answering service. This AI layer handles lead qualification, appointment scheduling, call routing, FAQ responses, and data collection. The AI Voice Agent requires an underlying Telephone Answering subscription starting at approximately **£160/month**, with total real-world costs typically reaching £300–£500/month once call volumes and out-of-hours coverage are factored in. Pricing is bespoke and not fully published; prospects must contact the sales team for a quote.

Moneypenny's model is built around large enterprise clients and established SMEs with sufficient call volume to justify a managed service. Its AI product is an add-on to human-staffed answering — not a natively AI-first, done-for-you automation stack.

### Secondary Candidate: ARROW (aiphonecalls.co.uk)

A closer direct competitor, and one operating specifically in the trades niche in the UK, is **ARROW** (trading as aiphonecalls.co.uk). ARROW is a UK-based done-for-you AI receptionist service explicitly targeting **plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, locksmiths, and landscapers**. Its AI receptionist answers calls 24/7, captures leads, qualifies prospects, and sends lead details directly to the owner's phone or inbox. There is no dashboard or login — the service is fully managed. Setup and training takes 3–7 days. Pricing starts from **£99/month**.

ARROW is the most direct UK analogue to SystemsWithOla's model, but at a lower price point and without the WhatsApp/SMS automation layer or the programme management depth that Ola brings. It appears to be a small operator rather than a scaled business.

### Also Worth Noting: Trademate (thetrademate.co.uk)

**Trademate** automates enquiries from Checkatrade, Rated People, MyBuilder, TrustATrader, and Bark.com, as well as social media, combining AI-powered lead replies, booking links, reminders, invoicing, and calls. The company claims clients see 3x more leads and 50% time savings on average. Pricing is not publicly listed; prospects are directed to book a call. Trademate's focus is on lead conversion from existing directory listings rather than inbound call answering — it is a complementary but different layer.

### The Gap SystemsWithOla Fills

Moneypenny is expensive, primarily enterprise-facing, and not built natively for trades. ARROW is affordable but thin — basic call answering without WhatsApp automation, follow-up sequences, or CRM-style workflow. Trademate handles leads from directories but not inbound phone calls. None of these combine:

- AI voice call answering (24/7 missed call capture)
- Instant WhatsApp/SMS owner notification
- Automated follow-up sequences to the lead
- Done-for-you setup and management with no technical burden on the trades owner
- A programme-managed, systemised back-office approach at a mid-market price point (£250–£450/month recurring)

SystemsWithOla sits in the gap between Moneypenny (too expensive, too enterprise) and the thin single-feature AI answering tools (too basic, no automation stack).

## Section 2: Market Size and Opportunity

### Number of Registered Trades Businesses in the UK

According to IBISWorld industry data (2024), there are:

- **48,993** registered electrician businesses in the UK (up 2.3% year-on-year)
- **44,630** registered plumbing, heating, and air conditioning installation businesses (up 2.9% year-on-year)

The broader construction and trades sector is the **largest sector by SME count** in the UK. According to the ONS Construction Statistics 2024, there were approximately **370,770 VAT/PAYE-registered construction firms** in Great Britain, with the number rising 1.7% in Q3 2024 versus Q3 2023. Including sole traders and micro-firms, the Statista/FSB data puts total construction and trades SMEs at approximately **870,000** in 2024 — representing roughly 16% of the entire UK SME population.

The FSB reports that sole traders and businesses with no employees make up approximately **74% of all UK SMEs**. Given that construction and trades are disproportionately sole-trader and micro-firm dominated, it is conservative to estimate that **over 85% of UK trades businesses** operate with fewer than 10 employees and no dedicated administrative function — making them the direct target for a done-for-you service.

### Revenue Lost to Missed Calls

A press release from Digital X Marketing (a UK digital marketing intelligence firm) directly targeting the trades sector states that **UK trades businesses — builders, electricians, and plumbers — lose an average of £24,000 per year in revenue due to missed phone calls**. For a typical sole trader or micro SME, missing one qualified lead per day results in losses exceeding £30,000 annually.

Paperclip (a UK call answering service) published research showing that UK businesses collectively lose an estimated **£30 billion** per year due to missed calls, equivalent to approximately **£5,500 per business** across the entire SME base. Given that trades businesses receive above-average inbound call volume relative to their staff capacity, the per-business figure for the trades sector is materially higher.

A separate analysis of 13,175 calls from 45 contractors (cited by Resonate AI and Oreate AI) found that **74.1% of calls went unanswered** during normal business hours, because staff were on job sites or unavailable. For roofing contractors specifically, the same study found an average of 87 inbound calls per month, with 76.6% going unanswered, and 10.6% of those unanswered calls being quote requests for projects averaging £15,000 — representing approximately **£252,000 in annual lost revenue per roofing business** at full opportunity cost.

Aira.io research corroborates this: **62% of inbound calls to home service businesses go unanswered**.

According to UBC Flexible Offices research, **almost half of UK SMEs** leave calls unanswered during normal business hours.

### AI Automation Adoption in UK SMEs

The British Chambers of Commerce (September 2025 survey) found that **35% of UK SMEs are actively using AI technology**, up from 25% in 2024 — a 40% year-on-year increase in active adoption. A separate YouGov/ProfileTree survey puts the figure at 45% having integrated at least one AI-based solution.

Critically, the trades and construction sector sits at the **bottom of the digital maturity curve**, alongside transport and hospitality. Under 20% of construction SMEs are estimated to use specialised digital tools beyond basic mobile devices. The UK government's Technology Adoption Review 2025 identified construction as a sector with "high growth potential but low AI maturity" — which is precisely why Innovate UK's BridgeAI programme listed construction as a target sector.

This creates a first-mover window. Trades businesses recognise the problem (missed calls, admin burden) but have not yet found an affordable, managed solution that requires zero technical knowledge to operate.

## Section 3: Competitive Landscape

### 1. Moneypenny (UK, Large Scale)

**Model:** Hybrid human + AI answering service. Clients get a dedicated PA or team who answer calls in the company's name, supplemented by an AI Voice Agent layer for out-of-hours and overflow. The AI product requires an underlying live-agent subscription.  
**Pricing:** From approximately £160/month for AI Voice Agent add-on; total typical cost £300–£500+/month depending on call volume. Enterprise pricing on application.  
**Target market:** SMEs and enterprise across all sectors. Trades are a subset, not the focus.  
**Gap vs SystemsWithOla:** No WhatsApp/SMS automation or follow-up sequences. Not trades-specialist. Expensive for a sole-trader electrician or plumber. No done-for-you back-office systemisation.

### 2. ARROW / AI Phone Calls (UK, Trades-Specific)

**Model:** Fully managed AI receptionist for UK trades businesses. No client dashboard. Answers calls 24/7, captures leads, sends details to the owner.  
**Pricing:** From **£99/month**. No setup fee mentioned.  
**Target market:** UK plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, locksmiths, landscapers.  
**Gap vs SystemsWithOla:** Lower price point but thinner product — no WhatsApp automation, no follow-up sequences, no CRM integration, no back-office workflow build. SystemsWithOla's £500 setup fee reflects a significantly deeper implementation and the ongoing £250–£450/month buys a full automation stack, not just call answering.

### 3. Voqal AI (UK, General SME)

**Model:** AI receptionist for UK businesses, 24/7 call answering, booking, and lead qualification. No-contract rolling monthly.  
**Pricing:** From **£197/month**.  
**Target market:** General UK SMEs including trades but not trades-specialist.  
**Gap vs SystemsWithOla:** No WhatsApp/SMS integration stated. Not done-for-you in the same total sense. No programme management layer.

### 4. Trademate (UK, Trades Lead Automation)

**Model:** Automates lead replies and booking from Checkatrade, Rated People, MyBuilder, TrustATrader, Bark.com, and social media. AI sends personalised emails with booking links, reminders, follow-ups, and handles invoicing.  
**Pricing:** Not publicly listed; contact required.  
**Target market:** UK tradespeople using lead generation directories.  
**Gap vs SystemsWithOla:** Trademate handles outbound automation from directory leads — it does not answer inbound phone calls. The two tools are complementary rather than directly competing.

### 5. ServiceTitan AI / ServiceAgent.ai (USA-originated, expanding to UK)

**Model:** Enterprise-grade field service management software with integrated AI voice agents (ServiceTitan's "Atlas" AI), booking, scheduling, CRM, invoicing, and dispatch. ServiceAgent is a standalone AI call answering agent for home services businesses with deep FSM integrations.  
**Pricing:** ServiceTitan is enterprise-level SaaS starting at several hundred pounds per month with significant onboarding requirements. ServiceAgent pricing is per-usage or flat subscription.  
**Target market:** Medium to large trades and home services contractors with established tech stacks.  
**Gap vs SystemsWithOla:** Too complex and expensive for UK sole traders and micro-SMEs. Requires significant technical onboarding. Not done-for-you in the UK market context. SystemsWithOla's value proposition is specifically for the owner who has no time, no technical knowledge, and no admin team.

## Section 4: Funding Opportunities

### 4.1 British Business Bank — Start Up Loans

The Start Up Loans scheme is the most accessible entry point. Loans are **£500 to £25,000 per person**, unsecured, at a **fixed rate of 7.5% per annum** (revised from 6% as of 6 April 2026). Repayment terms are one to five years. Multiple founders in the same business can each apply, with a maximum of £100,000 per business in total. The scheme was recently extended: businesses that have been trading for up to **60 months** are now eligible (previously 36 months). Loans come with free mentoring from a delivery partner. Applications are made through the Start Up Loans programme at startuploans.co.uk. This is a strong first option for SystemsWithOla given accessibility and no equity dilution.

### 4.2 Innovate UK — BridgeAI Innovation Exchange

Innovate UK's **BridgeAI** programme specifically targets sectors with **high growth potential and low AI maturity**, explicitly listing **construction** as a priority sector. The BridgeAI Innovation Exchange offers grants of **£25,000 to £50,000** for UK-registered SMEs to develop AI and machine learning proof-of-concepts addressing a business challenge or opportunity. Grants are awarded as Minimal Financial Assistance (MFA — formerly de minimis), so applicants must not have received more than £315,000 in such grants in the last three financial years. Future rounds should be monitored via apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk. This is highly relevant: a project framing SystemsWithOla's AI voice agent platform as an AI solution for the low-digital-maturity construction/trades sector is directly in scope.

### 4.3 Innovate UK — Smart Grants and AI/ML Feasibility

Innovate UK's broader **Smart Grants** competition is open to UK businesses in any sector, with awards typically **£25,000 to £500,000**. Separately, a recent competition offered a share of **£3 million** for SME feasibility studies in frontier AI and machine learning. These are more competitive and require a compelling R&D narrative. SystemsWithOla could frame a Smart Grant application around the novel application of multi-channel AI voice + WhatsApp automation in an underserved sector. Monitor current competitions at apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk.

### 4.4 Essex-Specific and Regional Support

The South East Midlands LEP (SEMLEP) has formally closed as a registered entity but its Growth Hub function continues, managing funding programmes and grant schemes for businesses in the area. The University of Essex offers **£10,000 innovation vouchers** for SMEs to engage with university expertise — relevant if Ola wishes to pursue an academic collaboration or R&D partnership to strengthen a grant application. The East of England has additional UKSPF (UK Shared Prosperity Fund) disbursements through local councils; Essex businesses should contact Essex County Council's business support team and the Business Essex, Southend, and Thurrock portal at southeastbusiness.org.uk for current grant listings.

For London-based activity, the Mayor of London's Small Business programme committed **£62 million** to support small businesses across the capital (announced 2025). The **Grow London Local** platform (growlondonlocal.london) is the gateway for London-based SME founders to access this support, including dedicated advice for Black entrepreneurs.

### 4.5 Funding for Black and Minority Founders

Several active programmes are directly relevant to Ola as a Black-founded UK tech business:

**Black Seed (blackseedvc.com):** A Black-led seed fund based in Brixton, London, investing only in Black entrepreneurs. Raised over £5 million, plans to invest in 30 Black-led startups by 2026. Pre-seed and seed stage. Relevant at early investment stage rather than grant stage, but worth an early relationship.

**F100 Growth Fund (Black Equity Organisation):** A partnership between BEO and Sky providing **up to £15,000** per business to Black entrepreneurs seeking to scale an existing MVP. The business must be Black-led, UK-registered, and growth-ready. Application windows are periodic — check blackequityorg.com.

**Digital Catapult Black Founders Programme:** An accelerator targeting pre-seed and seed stage Black-founded companies creating innovative digital products. Provides access to Digital Catapult's network, expertise, and competitive grant funding. Highly relevant given SystemsWithOla's AI/automation product — apply via digicatapult.org.uk.

**Hatch Enterprise:** An organisation supporting ethnic minority founders with programmes typically valued at £3,000+, including access to competitive grants for business growth. Cohort-based; apply at hatchenterprise.org.

**Ascend Investment Readiness Programme:** Supports early-stage, diverse-led London startups to access finance through a three-month programme. Directly relevant if Ola is preparing for a seed raise.

**Google Black Founders Fund:** Google's global programme for Black founders. While primarily US-focused historically, there is a UK track. Monitor startup.google.com for current cycles.

**OneTech / UK Black Tech:** Community and programme access rather than direct grant funding, but valuable for warm introductions to grant programmes, investors, and networks.

## Section 5: Key Statistics for the Business Plan

The following statistics are sourced and usable in a funding submission. Where a figure is unconfirmed or derives from a lower-credibility source, this is noted.

### Missed Calls and the Cost of Non-Response

- **62%** of inbound calls to UK home service businesses go unanswered. *(Source: Aira.io, corroborated by multiple industry studies)*
- **74.1%** of calls to tradespeople go unanswered during normal working hours, according to an analysis of 13,175 calls from 45 contractors. *(Source: Resonate AI / Oreate AI citing contractor call data)*
- UK trades businesses lose an average of **£24,000 per year** in revenue due to missed calls. *(Source: Digital X Marketing UK press release — note: this is a marketing company's own research; treat as indicative rather than academic)*
- UK businesses collectively lose an estimated **£30 billion** per year to missed calls, approximately £5,500 per business. *(Source: Paperclip.co.uk, a UK call answering service — proprietary research)*
- **85%** of potential customers will not leave a voicemail if their call goes unanswered. *(Source: widely cited; original source unconfirmed — treat as indicative)*
- **79%** of callers move to the next Google listing within 30 minutes if they have not spoken to a professional. *(Source: cited by Anthrova/industry sources — original primary source unconfirmed)*

### Speed-to-Lead Conversion

- Leads responded to within **5 minutes** are **21 times more likely to convert** than those contacted after 30 minutes. *(Source: InsideSales.com / XANT Lead Response Management study, analysis of 15,000+ leads)*
- Firms that contact a prospect within **one hour** of receiving an enquiry are **nearly 7 times more likely** to qualify the lead than those who wait longer. *(Source: Harvard Business Review, 2.24 million leads analysed)*
- Calling a lead within **1 minute** of enquiry results in a **391% increase** in conversion rates. *(Source: Velocify / ICE Mortgage Technology, 3.5 million leads)*
- The odds of qualifying a lead **drop 10x** after the first five minutes. *(Source: InsideSales.com / XANT — commonly cited; the 10x figure specifically relates to qualification odds, not the same as the 21x conversion figure above)*

### Market Scale

- Approximately **870,000 SMEs** operate in UK construction and trades — the largest sector by SME count in the UK (16% of all SMEs). *(Source: ONS / Statista Business Population Estimates 2024)*
- **48,993** electrician businesses registered in the UK (2024). *(Source: IBISWorld)*
- **44,630** plumbing, heating, and air conditioning businesses registered in the UK (2024). *(Source: IBISWorld)*
- **74%** of all UK SMEs are sole traders or businesses with no employees. *(Source: FSB / Business Population Estimates 2024)*
- Over **85%** of trades businesses are estimated to operate without dedicated admin or reception staff. *(Derived figure — conservative estimate based on FSB micro-business data and trades sector structure; not directly cited by a single source)*

### AI Adoption and the Digital Gap

- **35% of UK SMEs** are now actively using AI, up from 25% in 2024 — a 40% year-on-year growth in adoption. *(Source: British Chambers of Commerce, September 2025)*
- Only **26% of B2C firms** (the category most trades fall into) are using AI, versus 46% of B2B firms — the B2C and physical trades lag significantly. *(Source: British Chambers of Commerce 2025)*
- **30% of UK micro-businesses** (fewer than 10 employees) still hesitate to adopt AI, citing cost and complexity as the primary barriers. *(Source: ProfileTree analysis of British Chambers of Commerce data)*
- Innovate UK's BridgeAI programme explicitly identifies **construction as a priority sector** due to "high growth potential but low AI maturity." *(Source: Innovate UK BridgeAI competition documentation)*
- Only **25%** of UK construction firms use more than three specialised software applications across their projects. *(Source: Visibuild 2024 State of Digital Adoption in Construction)*

### SME Admin Burden

- UK small businesses spend an average of **33 hours per month** on administrative tasks — work that does not directly generate revenue. *(Source: Inkl / small business survey data)*
- Across the UK's 5.2 million SMEs, an estimated **624,000 hours per week** are consumed by non-revenue-generating admin. *(Source: Business Comparison / SME Web)*
- The average UK SME loses the equivalent of **71 working days per year** to admin, at an estimated annual cost of **£35,600**. *(Source: Sage UK research, 2025)*
- **49% of SME CEOs and COOs** spend four or more hours per week dealing with payment and invoicing administration alone. *(Source: SME survey data cited by BusinessComparison.com)*

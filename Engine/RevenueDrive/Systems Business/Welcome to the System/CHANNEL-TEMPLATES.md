## 7. OBJECTION HANDLERS (Use Anytime)

**"I don't have time for this."**

**"I already have a system."**
→ "Cool. What are you using?" [Listen. Then:] "Right. Does it book calls while you're on site?" [If no:] "That's what we do. Worth testing to see if it fits better?"

**"I'm not tech-savvy."**
→ "Neither am I. That's why I built it so a plumber could set it up without thinking about it. 30 minutes. We do it. You're live. That's the whole thing."

**"How much does it cost?"**
→ "Starter is £250/month. Growth is £350. Premium is £450. But here's what matters: the average contractor books 2-4 extra jobs per month. At your rate, that's 2-5k per month extra revenue. So the system pays for itself on the first job. Does that math work?"

→ "Smart. But here's the thing: the next missed call costs you money while you're thinking. What if we just test it for 7 days? Then you don't have to think—you'll just know. Fair?"

**"Your system won't work for my type of trade."**
→ "What trade are you?" [Listen.] "Perfect. That's actually what Gerry does. [Or: That's very similar to Gerry's work.] The system doesn't care whether you're HVAC, plumbing, or solar—it answers calls and captures info. That's universal."

## General Rules Across All Channels

1. **Lead with the pain** (missed calls, lost revenue)
2. **Solution in 2-3 sentences** (answers calls, texts you, follows up)
3. **Proof via Gerry** (every time)
4. **Your credibility** (highways operative, property manager, TfL)
5. **Low-friction next step** (free 7-day test, 15-min call)
6. **No jargon** (no "AI," no "voice agent," no "automation")

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Lead with "AI-powered" (they don't care about the tech)
❌ Talk about features (WhatsApp, email, voice) — talk about outcomes
❌ Make it sound complicated (it's not)
❌ Forget to mention Gerry (he's your proof)
❌ Soft close ("Let me know if you're interested") — strong close ("When's good for the test?")

---

## Final Thought

All of this works because it's built on truth:

- You DO understand the problem (9 years in highways)
- You HAVE built a system that works (Gerry proves it)
- You're NOT a typical vendor (your background shows why)
- You're willing to let them test (7 days free)

Lead with that. Everything else follows.

## Voice Pillars

### 1. Direct and grounded
Say the thing. Use a real example. Skip the warm-up. The audience is busy.

**Do:** "We lost a £3,000 job because no one answered the phone at 7pm on a Friday. That's not a people problem — that's a systems problem."

**Don't:** "In today's fast-paced business environment, it's more important than ever to have robust systems in place."

### 2. Vulnerable but constructive
The failure goes in. The lesson comes out. Never wallow — always point to the fix.

**Do:** "My business failed. Not because I wasn't working hard. Because I was juggling everything without a single system. I didn't even know that was the problem until it was over."

**Don't:** Skipping the failure. Or dwelling in it without the "here's what I know now" turn.

### 3. Premium, not flashy
Calm confidence. Quality visuals. Specific vocabulary. No hype language.

**Do:** "I've worked with 2-man bands and Tier 1 contractors. The difference wasn't talent. It was how they managed information."

**Don't:** "This will CHANGE YOUR LIFE." / "You WON'T BELIEVE this hack."

### 4. Community-led
Ask the question at the end. Invite the story. Spotlight others when possible.

**Do:** "What's the system that saved your business this year? Drop it below — someone in the comments needs it."

**Don't:** Broadcasting only. Never asking. Never inviting.

### 5. Faith-respecting
Values run through the work without preaching. Reference to purpose, standards, and integrity is natural.

**Do:** "I'm routing for your success regardless of whether you achieve it with me."

**Don't:** Forcing religious framing unless Ola explicitly chooses that angle for a specific post.

## Brand Vocabulary

### Use these words and phrases
- Systems, cadence, rhythm, repeatable, compound, friction, operational
- "Mechanical work" (the DevOps concept — tasks that should be automated)
- "Chaos into cashflow"
- Specific numbers: "72 hours", "£200", "5-day sprint", "8–10k followers"
- Real names and places when relevant: Ringways, Taylor Woodrow, Marlborough, HS2
- Credentials used as proof, not as flex: CDMP, PMP, AWS, 9 years, 11 projects

### Never use
- Em-dashes (--)
- "Here is the truth" / "this matters because" / "here is why this matters"
- "No fluff" / "straightforward" / "genuinely" / "honestly"
- Vague superlatives: "incredible", "amazing", "game-changing" (without a specific example)
- Corporate filler: "in today's landscape", "moving forward", "leverage synergies"
- AI-sounding openers: "Certainly!", "Great question!", "As an AI..."

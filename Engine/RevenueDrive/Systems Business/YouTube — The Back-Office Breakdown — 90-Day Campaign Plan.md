Yes — with one condition. The condition is voice.

When someone finds The Back-Office Breakdown and then finds Systems With Melly on Instagram, they recognise the same person. The channel works as a discovery layer. ## YouTube Partner Programme — What You Are Actually Targeting

**Standard YPP (long-form path):**
- 1,000 subscribers
- 4,000 public watch hours in the past 12 months

**YPP via Shorts (faster path):**
- 1,000 subscribers
- 10 million public Shorts views in 90 days

**The honest 90-day assessment:**
The Shorts path (10M views) is what gets creators monetised fastest. One viral Short can deliver it in a week. The long-form watch hours path is more predictable but slower — 4,000 watch hours across 13 videos in 90 days means approximately 18–20 minutes of average watch time per view at 1,000 views per video. That is achievable with quality content and consistent posting, but tight.

**The recommended strategy:** run both simultaneously. Shorts to sprint the subscriber count. Long-form to bank the watch hours. Cross-promote both so each piece of content feeds the other.

Airbnb hosts, property operators, DevOps/IT professionals curious about AI tools, and trades business owners who find the channel through specific search queries and subscribe when the content answers the exact thing they searched.

## AI Tools Stack — Faceless Automation Pipeline

This is the full production workflow from script to published video. Each tool has a specific role. Do not add tools until you have mastered this core stack.

### Script Generation
**Tool: Claude (this session)**
Prompt template: "Write a YouTube script for The Back-Office Breakdown channel. Title: [X]. Length: [8 minutes / 45 seconds]. Audience: small business owners and operators. Tone: direct, grounded, story-first — no corporate speak. Open with a real example or specific number. End with a question that invites comments. No em-dashes."

### Voice Over
**Tool: ElevenLabs**
Action: Clone YOUR OWN voice first. Record 5–10 minutes of yourself speaking naturally (not reading, just talking). Upload to ElevenLabs Professional Voice Clone. This is the most important step in the entire production process. A generic AI voice will undermine the channel. Your cloned voice will make it sound like you recorded it yourself.
Cost: ElevenLabs Creator plan (~$22/month) covers voice cloning and unlimited generation.

### Video — B-Roll and Visuals
**Tool 1: Higgsfield AI** — for cinematic AI-generated B-roll. Best for atmospheric, psychological visuals that match the documentary tone of The Back-Office Breakdown. Use for opening sequences and key narrative beats.

**Tool 2: Pexels (free) + Storyblocks (paid, ~$15/month)** — stock footage for workplace, construction, property, tech scenes. Covers the bread-and-butter B-roll between Higgsfield clips.

**Tool 3: Canva** — animated text overlays, lower-thirds, infographics for data points and statistics.

Do NOT use Synthesia. It creates a corporate-looking digital avatar presenter that conflicts with the documentary, psychological feel of this channel. It works for software training content. It does not work for business storytelling.

### Editing
**Tool: CapCut (free)** — primary editing tool. Fast, auto-captions, templates for Shorts. On your Mac already. Handles both the 45-second Shorts and the 8–15 minute long-form videos.

**Tool: Descript** — worth adding at month 2 once the workflow is running. Lets you edit video by editing the transcript (delete words, remove filler, rearrange sections). Saves 40–60% of editing time on long-form content.

### Thumbnails
**Tool: Canva** — keep a consistent template. Dark background. Bold text. One strong visual element. The Back-Office Breakdown aesthetic should feel like a Netflix documentary thumbnail — not clickbait, but psychologically interesting.

Thumbnail formula: [Specific number or loss] + [Surprising angle] + [Dark premium visual]
Example: "£2,000,000. One Bad Decision. Zero Accountability."

### Short-Form Repurposing
**Tool: Opus Clip** — paste in the long-form video link. It identifies the highest-engagement moments and auto-generates Shorts with captions and dynamic zoom. One long-form video becomes 3–5 Shorts automatically.

### SEO & Research
**Tool: TubeBuddy or VidIQ** — install the Chrome extension. Shows you keyword search volume, competition score, and what your ideal viewers are actually searching. Free tier covers what you need to start. Use before writing every title and description.

**Tool: Perplexity AI** — for fast research when building a script. "Give me 5 real examples of businesses that failed because of poor systems" — then verify and add your own angle.

### Scheduling
**Tool: YouTube Studio** (native, free) — schedule videos directly. No third-party tool needed at this stage.

## Risks and Mitigations

**Risk 1: The 90-day YPP timeline is tight**
Reality: 4,000 watch hours in 90 days requires consistent posting and good retention. If you miss the target, you are 30 days away from the next review. The channel is still being built and still serving the brand regardless of when monetisation kicks in.
Mitigation: prioritise Shorts — they can deliver 10M views faster than watch hours accumulate. One viral Short changes the timeline entirely.

**Risk 2: ElevenLabs voice does not sound natural enough**
Reality: generic AI voices erode trust. This is a real risk if the clone is not done properly.
Mitigation: spend 30 minutes recording a proper voice sample. Conversational speech, not scripted reading. Vary pace and tone naturally. Test it on 3 people before publishing.

**Risk 3: Posting rhythm breaks in week 3 or 4**
Reality: most channels fail at consistency, not quality.
Mitigation: batch-produce. Every Sunday, write 2 scripts. Every Monday, record voice for both. Have a 2-week content buffer before you publish anything. If you post Week 1, you should already have Week 3 content edited and ready.

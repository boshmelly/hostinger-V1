## LINKEDIN POSTS

**Post 1 — Industry Insight**
The trades sector in the UK is sitting on a structural problem that has nothing to do with skills or demand.

74.1 percent of calls to trade businesses go unanswered during working hours. Not because owners are disorganised. Because the same person cannot lay cable and answer the phone simultaneously. That is physics, not customer service failure.

The consequence is measurable. £24,000 per year in missed revenue on average. Per business. Across 870,000 SMEs in construction and trades, the aggregate loss runs into the tens of billions annually.

The technology to close that gap exists and has done so for several years. DHL uses it. Enterprise retailers use it. The local electrician in Essex has not had access to it at a price point that makes commercial sense.

Systems By Melly is changing that. Done-for-you AI automation for local businesses, from £295 a month, live within 24 hours.

The race is not between big businesses and small ones. It is between businesses with systems and businesses without them.

---

**Post 2 — Founder Perspective**
I spent nine years inside UK infrastructure delivery. Coordinating contractors, managing site communications, watching skilled people lose jobs because the admin side of the business was running behind the operational side.

The problem I kept seeing was not ability. It was systems. Or the absence of them.

A four-person electrical team cannot answer every call, follow up every enquiry, maintain a social presence, and manage bookings while they are on site completing a job. Something always fell through.

Systems By Melly is built from that observation. Every piece of automation we implement solves a gap I watched skilled business owners struggle with for nearly a decade.

The businesses winning today are not necessarily better at their trade. They are better supported by the infrastructure running behind it.

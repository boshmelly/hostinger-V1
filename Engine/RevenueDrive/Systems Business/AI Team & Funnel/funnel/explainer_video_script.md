# Explainer Video Script — 60 to 90 seconds

## Goal

Drive viewer to the AI assistant call CTA on the landing page. One CTA, one path.

## Format

- 60 to 90 seconds
- Recorded on iPhone, locked tripod, natural light or single softbox
- Ola on camera as the speaker
- B-roll cuts from highways, property, Airbnb archive (real footage Ola already owns)
- Captions burned in for silent viewing on Facebook and Instagram
- 9:16 vertical version for Reels and Stories. 16:9 horizontal for landing page and YouTube.

## Script (read in Ola's voice)

### Cold open (0 to 5 seconds)

(Direct to camera, no intro music, low energy intentional)

"Most UK businesses are leaking £15,000 a month. They just can't see it."

(Cut to leakage diagram on screen, animated)

### Failure montage (5 to 25 seconds)

(B-roll: a phone ringing on a job site. A trade walking past it. Cut to a quote sitting in a folder. Cut to an inbox with 47 unread emails.)

Voiceover (Ola):

"The phone rings at 7pm on a Friday. Nobody answers. That's a £3,000 job gone."

"You send a quote. You don't follow up. 60% of deals close after the 5th touch. Most owners stop at the 1st."

"You spend 12 hours a week on admin a 17-year-old could automate. That's £45,000 of leaked time a year."

### The audit reveal (25 to 50 seconds)

(Back to Ola on camera)

"I built RevenueDrive to find this leakage and prove it. We run a 14-day audit across four areas. Lead capture. Quote conversion. Ops handoffs. Admin efficiency."

(Cut to a redacted audit page on screen, scrolling)

"You get a leakage map. A recovery plan. ROI math signed off by me."

### The promise (50 to 65 seconds)

(Back to Ola on camera, close-up)

"If we find under £15,000 of recoverable revenue, you owe us nothing."

(Beat)

"That's not a marketing line. That's the deal."

### CTA (65 to 85 seconds)

(Cut to landing page hero on screen, with the AI assistant CTA highlighted)

Voiceover (Ola):

"Talk to our AI assistant. It'll ask you 6 questions about your business, tell you whether we can help, and book you in if we can."

"Link in the description. Brochure on the page. No sales call until you decide you want one."

### Sign-off (85 to 90 seconds)

(Back to Ola, slight smile)

"Systems With Melly. RevenueDrive. We find the leak. You decide what to do."

(Cut)

## Production notes

- Ola wears a clean dark shirt, no logo. Reference: the existing premium aesthetic in `/Brand Assets - SystemsByMelly/`.
- Audio: Lavalier mic, not phone mic. Critical.
- Edit: Descript or CapCut. Captions in font matching brand (Inter or similar).
- Title card and end card with logo and URL.
- No background music behind voice. Subtle ambient bed under B-roll only.

## Voice rules check

- No em-dashes (none in script)
- No banned words (review against `/brand_voice_melly.md`)
- Specific numbers: 3 of them
- Failure stories: 3 of them
- Direct CTA: 1
- Length: 90 seconds max

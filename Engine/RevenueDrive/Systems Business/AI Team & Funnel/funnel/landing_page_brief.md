# Landing Page Brief — RevenueDrive Audit

## Outcome anchor

"We find £15,000+ in leaking revenue across your lead capture, ops handoffs, and follow-up flow in 14 days. If we don't, you pay nothing."

## URL

revenuedrive.co.uk (or systemswithmelly.com/revenuedrive). PM to lock domain in Week 1.

## Build platform

Wix or Webflow. Wix preferred for speed and Ola's existing familiarity. Webflow if a partner is willing to build Week 1.

## Animation rule

Scroll-triggered scenes only. No carousels, no hero video autoplay, no chrome flying around. Smooth, calm, premium. Reference: Linear.app, Stripe, Notion landing pages.

## Page structure (top to bottom)

### 1. Hero (above the fold)

Headline (3 options for Marketing to test):
- "Find £15,000 of leaking revenue in your business in 14 days. Or pay nothing."
- "The audit that pays for itself before you pay for it."
- "Stop guessing where revenue is leaking. We find it. We prove it. You decide what to do."

Subhead: "RevenueDrive runs a 14-day audit of your lead capture, ops handoffs, and follow-up flow. We deliver the leakage map, the recovery plan, and the ROI math. If we find under £15k, you owe us nothing."

Primary CTA: "Talk to our AI assistant. Get assessed."
Secondary CTA: "Download the brochure."

Hero visual: short scroll-in animation showing a leakage diagram (lead → quote → follow-up → close, with red leaks at each junction). No people. No stock photos.

### 2. The Problem

Headline: "Most businesses are leaking revenue daily. They just can't see it."

Three failure modes, each with a specific cost figure:

- Missed calls: "The average UK trades business misses 30% of calls during business hours. At £400 average job value, that's £12,000 per month walking away."
- Quote drop-off: "Most service businesses send a quote and never follow up. 60% of deals close after the 5th touch. Most owners stop at the 1st."
- Admin time leakage: "An owner doing 12 hours a week of repeatable admin loses 624 hours a year. At £75 an hour, that's £46,800 of leaked time."

Source citations under each (link to the underlying study or methodology). Builds credibility.

### 3. What We Do

Headline: "The 14-day RevenueDrive Audit."

The 4-stage framework (one icon, one line each):

1. Lead Capture audit — every channel, every drop-off
2. Quote Conversion audit — speed, follow-up, win rate
3. Ops Handoffs audit — where information dies between people
4. Admin Efficiency audit — what should be automated, what shouldn't

Single sentence under: "You get a leakage map, a recovery plan, and an ROI projection signed by Ola. If recovery is under £15k, the audit is free."

### 4. Proof

Headline: "Early case studies. Redacted, not embellished."

3 case-style breakdowns even if they're early. Format:
- Industry, size, what we found (number), what we recommended, what they recovered.
- Use anonymous handles ("UK-based 8-van electrical contractor") if real names not yet permissioned.

### 5. Who This Is For (and Who It Isn't)

Two columns. Honest.

For:
- UK SMEs with £500k to £10m turnover
- Service businesses (trades, M&E, estate agents, manufacturing SMEs, dealerships)
- Owners who already know their systems are messy
- Owners who want clarity before spending more on ads or staff

Not for:
- Pre-revenue businesses
- Pure ecommerce (DTC, subscription)
- Startups looking for fundraising consultancy
- Anyone shopping for the cheapest option

### 6. About Ola

Photo. One paragraph. Credibility stack: CDMP, PMP, AWS DevOps (11 projects), 9 years highways and street works (operative to programme coordinator), residential property and Airbnb operator, business owner who failed and rebuilt with systems.

One line: "I've been on jobs with you. I've run a business that failed without systems. I know exactly where revenue leaks because I've leaked it myself."

### 7. CTA repeat

Same as hero. Bigger button. AI assistant first.

### 8. FAQ

5 questions max:
- How do you find £15k of leakage?
- What if you don't?
- What does the audit cost?
- How long does it take?
- Do you implement, or just report?

### 9. Footer

Privacy, terms, contact. No social links yet (until 30-day sprint hits target).

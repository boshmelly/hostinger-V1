# Brochure PDF Outline — RevenueDrive Audit

## Why a brochure works for this offer

Three reasons:

1. £3k+ B2B sales involve 2 to 3 people. The owner forwards a PDF to a partner, accountant, or ops director. A landing page can't do that job.
2. Boomer-owned and 45+ owners trust artifacts they can print. Half your target market still keeps physical files.
3. The Porsche brochure logic: thick reasoning, clean visuals, one unmistakable promise. At SME scale, the principle holds.

## Spec

- 8 to 12 pages, A4 portrait
- Designed in Canva or Figma. Export as PDF, optimised under 5MB.
- Branded under "Revenue Drive, delivered by Systems With Melly"
- Cover and inside pages: clean white, one accent colour (deep navy or burnt orange), one font (Inter or similar), specific numbers in larger weight.
- No stock photos of handshakes. Real diagrams, real screenshots, real numbers.

## Page-by-page

### Page 1 — Cover

- Logo
- Headline: "We find £15,000+ in leaking revenue in 14 days. Or you pay nothing."
- Subhead: "The RevenueDrive Audit, for UK service businesses with £500k to £10m turnover."
- One small visual: leakage diagram (same as landing page hero)

### Page 2 — The honest opening

Headline: "If you're guessing where money is leaking, you're already losing it."

One paragraph. Direct. No throat-clearing. Names the reader's reality.

Three bullets with specific costs (same as landing page Problem section):
- Missed calls cost
- Quote follow-up cost
- Admin time cost

### Page 3 — The 4-stage audit framework

Diagram. Lead Capture > Quote Conversion > Ops Handoffs > Admin Efficiency. Each stage with 3 to 5 sub-checks.

One sentence under: "Same sequence every time. No improvisation. No hourly billing surprises."

### Page 4 — Sample insight 1 (real, redacted)

Format: industry, size, finding, recommendation, recovery estimate, signature.

Example layout:
- Industry: 8-van electrical contractor, £1.4m turnover
- Finding: 31% of inbound calls unanswered between 5pm and 7pm. Estimated £4,200 per month walked.
- Recommendation: AI receptionist + booking flow on GHL.
- Recovery estimate: £50,400 first-year revenue recovered.
- Implementation cost: £450 (Tier 2 build)

### Page 5 — Sample insight 2 (different industry)

Same format. Different industry from page 4.

### Page 6 — ROI math, no spin

Side-by-side table:
- Audit fee: £3,000
- Median recovery from audits: £18k to £45k first 90 days (update as data accumulates)
- Implementation: optional, £450 to £5,000 depending on tier
- Net first-year position: positive in 8 of last 10 audits (update with real data)

If you don't have data yet, say "data updated quarterly, current as of [month]" and use defensible projections from industry benchmarks.

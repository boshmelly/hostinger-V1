# £300 Validation Test Plan

## Purpose

Validate three things only:

1. Does the offer trigger engagement?
2. Does the AI assistant call get booked?
3. What do prospects honestly say they'd pay?

This is not a sales push. No revenue target. Data only.

## Budget

£300 total. Split as £75 per industry across 4 industries (Construction subs, Estate agencies, M&E, Dealerships). Manufacturing and the secondary 3 wait for Week 4 if budget remains.

## Channel

Facebook Ads + Instagram (paired, same creative). LinkedIn parked for Week 4 unless results are weak.

## Duration

7 days. Hard cap.

## Creative split per industry

Same offer, same landing page, same brochure. Swap headline only.

### Construction subs
- "Find £15k of leaking revenue in your subcontracting business in 14 days. Or pay nothing."

### Estate agencies
- "Find £15k of leaking revenue in your agency in 14 days. Or pay nothing."

### M&E specialist
- "Find £15k of leaking revenue in your firm in 14 days. Or pay nothing."

### Dealerships
- "Find £15k of leaking revenue in your dealership in 14 days. Or pay nothing."

## Three offer variants per industry (within the £75)

A. AI Opportunity Snapshot (lead magnet, no audit fee mentioned in the ad)
B. Free SEO / visibility audit (lead magnet, gateway to RevenueDrive Audit)
C. Brochure download (lead magnet, gateway to AI assistant call)

Within each industry's £75, split £25 / £25 / £25 across A / B / C. After 48 hours, kill the worst-performing variant in each industry and pour into the leader.

## Tracking and capture

### Required events on landing page

- Page view (with industry tag from URL parameter)
- Brochure form submit (capture email + industry)
- AI assistant call started
- AI assistant call completed
- Willingness-to-pay band selected
- Discovery call booked

All firing into Facebook Pixel, GA4, and GHL.

## Kill rules

- Any ad with cost-per-form-submit over £30 after £40 spend: cut
- Any industry with zero AI assistant calls after £75 total: deprioritise for next test
- Any creative with sub-1% CTR after £20 spend: cut
- Any landing page bounce rate over 75% on a specific industry: investigate, fix copy, don't keep pouring

## Reporting cadence

Day 1: Setup verification. All pixels firing. All lead capture working. Deliverability checked.
Day 3: Mid-test read. Cut bottom 3 ad variants. Document what's running.
Day 7: Final read. Full data dump to PM.

## Final report (Research agent owns)

One page. Contains:

1. Cost per booking by industry
2. Willingness-to-pay distribution from AI assistant calls (count by band)
3. Industry conversion ranking
4. Top performing creative angle
5. Recommendation: which 2 industries to scale, which to retire, what to test next

## What this test does NOT prove

- It does not prove the £3k audit will sell. It proves whether the market engages with the promise.
- It does not prove the AI assistant works at scale. Volume is too low.
- It does not prove conversion rate. £300 is too small a sample for that.

## What this test DOES prove

- Whether the message lands at all
- Which industry is cheapest to acquire
- What pricing band the market actually expects
- Where the AI assistant breaks (drop-off points)

## Owner

Marketing Strategist runs. Research analyses. Brand Voice approves all creative. PM signs off before launch and signs off the final report.

# Boomer-Owned and 45+ Owner Overlay

## Why this overlay matters for RevenueDrive

The audit pricing (£3k+) and the outcome promise (recovered revenue) only land with owners who have:

- Real cash flow (not pre-revenue)
- Real systems debt (not greenfield)
- Real authority to spend without committee
- Real pain that has festered long enough to be expensive

That description matches owner-led UK businesses where the owner is 45+ and frequently 55+.

## What the data confirms

- Most UK business owners are aged 55+, with limited succession planning (16% have no plan, 69% have no documented plan)
- Family firms most common in construction and primary sectors
- Top AI-adoption barriers in this segment: skills gap (35%), cost (30%), ROI uncertainty (25%), and cultural resistance to change

Source: GOV.UK AI Adoption Research, Armstrong Watson 2025, STEP

## The behavioural signature

Owners 45+ in target industries tend to:

1. Trust artifacts (PDF brochures, printed reports) over digital-only
2. Defer to a partner, accountant, or ops lead before signing
3. Prefer phone calls over chat
4. Resist tech demos but respond to ROI math
5. Value long-tenure credentials (CDMP, PMP, "9 years on highways") more than younger cohorts do
6. Listen to peer recommendations and trade body endorsements
7. Hate being "sold to" but respond to direct, calm, specific language

## How this changes the funnel

### Landing page

- Brochure download CTA prominently placed alongside AI assistant CTA
- About page references long-tenure credentials early
- Photo of Ola, professional, calm
- Reduce animation aggression, prioritise calm scroll
- Phone number visible, not buried

### Brochure

- Print-friendly layout (already specified)
- Page 9 about-page leans into the credibility stack hard
- ROI math gets a dedicated page (page 6)
- Trade body affiliations and contractor names where permitted

### Explainer video

- Tone slightly slower than typical TikTok pacing
- Captions essential (assume the volume is off)
- Ola in business-casual, not casual
- Authority signal in the first 3 seconds (credentials briefly on screen)

### AI assistant

- Identifies as AI immediately (no deception)
- Offers a human handoff fast (2 questions in)
- Captures phone number for human callback option
- Polite, calm, no slang

### Ads

- Static-image and carousel formats outperform reels for this cohort
- LinkedIn outperforms TikTok for cold reach in 45+ owner segment
- Facebook still works for trades and dealerships specifically

## Targeting parameters (Facebook Ads, Week 2)

### Audience 1 — Construction subs

- Location: UK
- Age: 45 to 65
- Job titles: Owner, Director, Managing Director, Founder
- Industries: Construction, Civil Engineering, Building Services
- Interests: Trade body affiliations (FMB, NICEIC, NAPIT), trade publications
- Exclusions: Employees of the above firms (we want owners only)

### Audience 3 — Manufacturing SME owners

- Interests: Make UK, MTA, manufacturing trade press

### Audience 4 — Independent dealership owners

- Location: UK
- Age: 45 to 65
- Job titles: Dealer Principal, Owner, MD
- Industries: Automotive
- Interests: NFDA, Auto Trader (business)

## Messaging adjustments for 45+ owners

### Use

- "I've been on the jobs"
- Specific year counts (9 years, 11 projects, 15 years in UK)
- "Founder-led, UK-based"
- "We send you a signed projection"
- "Phone us if you'd rather"

### Avoid

- "Hack", "growth", "10x", "scale"
- Emoji-heavy copy
- Trendy buzzwords
- "AI everything" framing (we're an AI service but we don't sell AI, we sell recovered revenue)

## Ola's natural advantage with this cohort

- Mid-30s but with 9 years' highways experience reads as "old-school work ethic in a younger frame"
- CDMP and PMP are vocabulary boomer owners recognise as serious
- "Worked with 2-man bands and Tier 1 contractors" signals he's been on the ground
- The failure-then-systems story plays well with owners who have lived through their own messy years

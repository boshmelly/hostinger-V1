# Week 1 to 3 Execution Plan

## North star

By end of Week 3:
- Landing page live
- Brochure v1 published
- Explainer video released
- AI assistant taking calls
- £300 validation test complete
- 5+ assessment calls booked with real prospects
- Decision data on which industry and price band wins

## Week 1 — Build the foundation

### Day 1 (Thursday 30 April 2026)
- PM: load this folder into the Agent stack working memory. Brief each agent on their mission.
- Ola: confirm domain (revenuedrive.co.uk available?), Wix vs Webflow choice, AI assistant tool choice (Vapi or Bland)
- Research: pull initial 25 prospect list across top 5 industries

### Day 2
- Marketing: draft 3 landing page hero variants, 12 ad copy variants, 5 brochure cover hooks
- Brand Voice: review all drafts, send back rewrites
- Product / Ops: lock the audit framework v1 (already drafted, validate)

### Day 3 to 4
- Marketing: finalise landing page wireframe
- Product / Ops: brochure content draft (12 pages), AI assistant prompt v1
- Research: prospect list complete, industry signals report

### Day 5 to 7
- Build phase. Whoever's executing builds the landing page (Ola or implementer).
- Brochure designed in Canva (Ola or implementer).
- AI assistant built and tested with internal calls.
- Ola records explainer video.

### End of Week 1 deliverables (PM audits)
- Landing page rough cut, password-protected
- Brochure v1 PDF, draft
- Explainer video raw cut
- AI assistant tested with 5 internal calls
- Prospect list ready
- All 4 ad creative sets ready in Facebook Ads Manager

## Week 2 — Ship and test

### Day 8 to 9
- PM: full audit of Week 1 deliverables. Anything failing the outcome anchor or voice rules: fix or scrap.
- Marketing: launch the £300 validation test
- Brand Voice: final approval on every piece going live

### Day 10 to 11
- Ads running, daily monitoring
- AI assistant taking real calls (low volume initially)
- Research: daily ad performance read, hourly check on AI assistant logs

### Day 12 to 14
- Mid-test cuts (kill bottom 3 ad variants based on Day 3 data)
- Marketing: refine top 2 angles, push budget into them
- Product / Ops: any AI assistant fixes (drop-off points, prompt tweaks)
- Brochure v1 distributed to anyone who downloads

### End of Week 2 deliverables (PM audits)
- Test data flowing daily into reporting
- 10+ AI assistant call logs
- 2+ assessment calls booked with Ola
- Email sequence active for brochure downloaders
- Mid-test verdict shared with Ola

## Week 3 — Read, refine, decide

### Day 15 to 17
- £300 test ends. Research compiles full report.
- Willingness-to-pay distribution analysed.
- Industry winner identified.
- Top 3 angles ranked.
- Drop-off points in the funnel mapped.

### Day 18 to 19
- PM compiles decision pack for Ola
- Recommendation: which industry to lead with, what price band to anchor, what to scrap, what's next
- Ola makes calls

### Day 20 to 21
- First 5 assessment calls held with real prospects
- Audit framework v1 tested on 1 to 2 paying prospects (or pilot at reduced fee)
- Feedback loop: what worked in the discovery call, what didn't

### End of Week 3 deliverables (PM audits, Ola signs)
- Validated offer (or pivot decision)
- Pricing locked based on data
- Industry focus locked (top 1 or 2)
- Scaled ad budget proposal for Week 4 onwards
- Audit framework v1.1 (revised based on first real runs)

## What gets cut if behind schedule

In order of cuttable:
1. Email sequence beyond Email 1 (delay to Week 4)
2. Multiple ad creative variants (run 2 instead of 4 per industry)
3. AI assistant voice version (fall back to Typeform + Calendly for Week 1, upgrade Week 2)
4. Brochure pages 5 to 6 sample insights (use placeholder if data not ready)

What does NOT get cut:
- The outcome anchor in every asset
- Brand Voice review on customer-facing copy
- The £300 ad test
- The AI assistant itself (in some form)

## Roles, locked

- **Ola**: Discovery calls, video on-camera, final go/no-go on every ship, the brand. Maximum 4 hours / day on this build (preserve sales time and content time).
- **Research agent**: Prospect lists, ad data, willingness-to-pay analysis
- **Brand Voice agent**: Reviews everything customer-facing
- **Marketing Strategist agent**: Ads, angles, copy variants, kill rules
- **Product / Ops agent**: Audit framework, AI assistant, brochure content, delivery SOPs
- **PM Hub agent**: Orchestration, audit, only ships decision-ready briefs to Ola

## Implementer engagement

- Landing page build: outsource to vetted implementer (Nigeria or India network), 3-day sprint, £200 to £400
- Brochure design: outsource to Canva specialist, 2-day sprint, £100 to £200
- Explainer video edit: outsource if Ola doesn't want to learn Descript, £150 to £250

## Total Week 1 to 3 cost (excluding ad spend)

- Implementer fees: ~£600 to £900
- Tools: Wix (£20), GHL (already paid), Brevo (free tier), Vapi or Bland (£50 to £150 for test volume), Canva (£15)
- Ad spend: £300
- Total: ~£985 to £1,415

## Owner

PM Hub agent maintains this plan. Updates after every weekly audit. Ola gets the Monday Brief, nothing else.

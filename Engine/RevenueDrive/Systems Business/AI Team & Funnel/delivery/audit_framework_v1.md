# Audit Framework v1 — RevenueDrive 14-Day Audit

## The promise this framework must keep

Find £15,000+ in leaking revenue across the prospect's lead capture, ops handoffs, and follow-up flow within 14 days. If under £15k, the audit fee is refunded.

This framework is the repeatable engine that makes the promise defensible. Same sequence every audit. No improvisation. The audit is product, not consultation.

## The 4 stages

Same order, every audit. Each stage produces a defined output and a leakage figure.

### Stage 1 — Lead Capture audit (Day 1 to 3)

**What we measure**

- Inbound channels (calls, web forms, social DMs, email, walk-ins, referrals)
- Response time to first contact
- Missed-call rate (split by hour of day, day of week)
- Form submission completion rate
- Channel attribution (does the owner know where leads come from?)

**Data sources**

- Phone system call logs (or carrier records)
- Website analytics (GA4 or platform default)
- CRM lead source data (or absence thereof)
- Social platform message counts
- 7-day mystery-shopper test (we call, email, and form-submit ourselves and time the response)

**Output**

- Leakage figure 1: £ value of unanswered or slow-response leads (calculated from volume × close rate × average value × % missed)
- Severity score 0 to 25

### Stage 2 — Quote Conversion audit (Day 4 to 6)

**What we measure**

- Quote turnaround time (enquiry to quote sent)
- Quote follow-up cadence (touches per quote)
- Win rate by source, by ticket size
- Drop-off point in the quote-to-decision journey
- Pricing pattern (discount frequency, margin variance)

**Data sources**

- CRM (or paper records, spreadsheets)
- Email logs
- Sample of 30 to 50 recent quotes (we read them)
- Owner interview about top 5 lost deals last quarter

**Output**

- Leakage figure 2: £ value of recoverable wins (calculated from quotes-sent × average value × estimated lift from systematic follow-up)
- Severity score 0 to 25

### Stage 3 — Ops Handoffs audit (Day 7 to 10)

**What we measure**

- Information flow between sales, scheduling, delivery, and admin
- Document handoff (where does customer info live? Who duplicates it?)
- Job-site to office communication delay
- Variation tracking (changes after the quote)
- Customer touchpoint count from booking to invoice

**Data sources**

- Job folders or digital records (last 5 closed jobs)
- Owner and team interviews (45 min)
- Software stack inventory
- Workflow walk-through (we shadow one job from booking to invoice)

**Output**

- Leakage figure 3: £ value of delivery friction (calculated from time-to-cash delay, error correction cost, customer churn from poor experience)
- Severity score 0 to 25

### Stage 4 — Admin Efficiency audit (Day 11 to 13)

**What we measure**

- Hours spent on repetitive admin (owner + team)
- Tasks that should be automated but aren't
- Duplicate data entry instances
- Missed compliance or follow-through (warranty, certification, customer satisfaction)
- Reporting and visibility (does the owner know their numbers in real-time?)

**Data sources**

- Time-tracking exercise for 5 working days (team logs admin time)
- Software audit (what's bought, what's used, what's wasted)
- Sample of last week's admin emails

**Output**

- Leakage figure 4: £ value of admin time at owner's effective hourly rate
- Severity score 0 to 25

## Synthesis (Day 14)

### Total leakage calculation

Sum of figures 1 to 4. If total >= £15,000 first-year recovery: pass.
If total < £15,000: refund. Document why (genuine outlier or audit error). Update framework.

### Total severity score

Sum of severity scores 0 to 100. Below 60 = high-recovery business. 60 to 80 = moderate. Above 80 = audit was inefficient or business is rare-low-leakage.

### Findings presentation (60 min, Ola live)

- Executive summary (one slide, total leakage figure)
- Stage-by-stage findings (4 slides, one per stage)
- Top 3 priority fixes ranked by ROI
- Recommended next step (Tier 1 / 2 / 3 build, or DIY playbook if low budget)
- ROI projection signed by Ola

### Deliverable pack

- Audit report (PDF, branded)
- Leakage map (visual diagram)
- Priority action list (top 10 fixes ranked by impact)
- ROI projection (signed)
- Recommended implementation tier with quote
- Optional: 30-day check-in call (free)

## Quality gates

- Every audit reviewed by PM before findings presentation
- Brand Voice reviews all written deliverables
- Implementer pack created if client buys implementation tier

## What this framework does NOT do

- Promise specific recovery for every audit. The £15k is a floor, not a guarantee of upside.
- Sell implementation. Ola decides whether to recommend Tier 1 / 2 / 3 based on findings.
- Run the audit on businesses outside the qualified list. Pre-audit screening matters.

## Iteration plan

After 5 audits: review the framework. Adjust scoring. Adjust time budget. Lock v2.
After 20 audits: industry-specific framework variants (one for trades, one for estate agencies, etc.)
After 50 audits: package the framework as a productised offering with a fixed pricing card per industry.

## Owner

Product / Ops agent owns the framework. PM audits every audit run. Ola owns the findings presentation and the recommendation.

# lead_generation_strategy.md: Turning Attention Into Calls

## The funnel
Content (attention) → Profile (positioning) → Lead magnet (free AI audit) → Call (qualify) → Offer.

## Step 1: Content that pulls the right people
Lead with Pillar 1 (AI for real businesses) aimed at construction. Every demo answers a problem a trades owner feels. The right viewer thinks "that is my business."

## Step 2: Profile that converts the click
- Bio states who you help and the outcome.
- Featured/pinned: the free AI audit offer.
- One link: a simple booking page with a qualifier.

## Step 3: The lead magnet (Phase 1 offer)
Free, high-value, low-effort to deliver at first:
- **AI Readiness Assessment** or **AI Opportunity Report.**
- 5-10 question form. Output: a 1-page report on where AI saves them time.
- Position it as the audit, not a sales call.

## Step 4: Qualify before you book (protect your time)
Booking form asks:
- Business type and size
- Biggest time drain
- Monthly revenue band
- Are they the decision maker
Only decision makers in your target bands get a call slot. Everyone else gets the report and a nurture sequence.

## Step 5: The call
20-30 minutes. Diagnose one bottleneck. Show one fix. If fit, present a Phase 2 offer. If not, give value and ask for a referral.

## Inbound sources (in priority order)
1. LinkedIn content + DMs from warm commenters.
2. Comments-to-DM: anyone who engages twice gets a soft DM.
3. Referrals from early clients.
4. Local trades groups and associations where you already have standing.

## Outbound to prime the pump (first 60 days)
- 5 personalised DMs a day to target trades owners. No pitch. Lead with a useful observation or offer the free audit.
- Comment with substance on 5 target accounts daily.

## Weekly targets
- 5 qualified inbound conversations
- 5 booked calls
- 25 outbound touches

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Put a hard qualifier in front of the booking link from day one.
**Evidence:** Your time is split across a day job, delivery, and content.
**Reason:** Unqualified calls are the biggest hidden cost in early consulting. A qualifier trades volume for fit and protects your scarcest resource.
**Point:** Better to do 5 right calls than 19 random ones.

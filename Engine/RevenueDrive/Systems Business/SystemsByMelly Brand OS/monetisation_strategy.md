# monetisation_strategy.md: How The Brand Makes Money

Three phases. Move a client up the ladder, do not start at the top.

## Phase 1: Attention and trust (free)
**Offer:** Free AI audit / AI readiness assessment / AI opportunity report.
**Goal:** Build pipeline and prove value before asking for money.
**Price:** £0.
**Delivery:** 1-page report + short call.

## Phase 2: Low-ticket implementation (cash flow)
**Offers:**
- Website systems
- Lead capture systems
- AI assistants
- CRM optimisation
**Price band:** £500-£2,500 per project.
**Goal:** Fast wins, testimonials, proof, recurring referrals.
**Why:** Easy yes, quick delivery, builds case studies that sell Phase 3.

## Phase 3: High-ticket services (profit)
**Offers:**
- Fractional AI Partner (retainer)
- AI implementation strategy
- Business systems consulting
- Governance and compliance advisory
**Price band:** £2,000-£5,000+ per month or per engagement.
**Goal:** Stable, high-margin revenue. This is the destination.

## Path to £6k/month (the math)
Pick the simplest mix, not the biggest:
- **Option A:** 2 Phase 3 retainers at £3k = £6k.
- **Option B:** 1 retainer at £3k + 2 Phase 2 projects at £1.5k = £6k.
- **Option C:** 4 Phase 2 projects at £1.5k = £6k (more delivery load).
Option A is the lowest-effort, highest-leverage route.

## The ladder in practice
Free audit reveals a bottleneck → small paid fix (Phase 2) proves you deliver → retainer (Phase 3) to run their systems ongoing.

## Pricing rules
- Anchor on outcome and time saved, not hours.
- Never discount the retainer. Discount scope instead.
- Always name a price on the call. Silence kills deals.

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Use Phase 2 as the on-ramp, but aim every client at a Phase 3 retainer.
**Evidence:** £6k/month from 2 retainers is far less delivery work than from 4 one-off projects.
**Reason:** Retainers are leverage. They compound, reduce sales effort, and free your time for content and growth.
**Point:** Sell projects to earn trust, sell retainers to build the business.

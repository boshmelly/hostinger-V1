# content_pillars.md: Three Pillars

Three areas only. Construction, Business, AI Systems benefits. Everything you post maps to one. If it does not, you do not post it.

## Pillar 1: AI For Real Businesses
**Goal:** Generate leads.
**Promise:** Show SMEs how to use AI practically, without the hype.
**Topics:**
- AI tools worth paying for, and tools to avoid
- How I would automate a specific local business (electrician, solar, EV)
- Real AI workflows on screen
- AI audits and what they reveal
- Implementation lessons and mistakes
- EU AI Act explained simply
**Format bias:** screen recordings and demos.

## Pillar 2: Building In Public
**Goal:** Connection and trust.
**Promise:** Document the real journey of building an AI systems consultancy.
**Topics:**
- Building RevenueDrive and AI assistants
- First clients and how they came
- Wins, failures, experiments
- What worked, what wasted money
**Format bias:** short commentary, occasional talking head.

## Pillar 3: Business Systems
**Goal:** Authority.
**Promise:** Most businesses have a process problem before an AI problem.
**Topics:**
- Common bottlenecks in small businesses
- Operational mistakes that cost money
- Lessons from infrastructure delivery, scaled down
- Systems thinking, governance, automation readiness
**Format bias:** commentary, frameworks, demos.

## Your three named focus areas mapped
- **Construction:** lives mainly in Pillar 1 (automating trades) and Pillar 3 (site/ops systems). This is your warm market.
- **Business:** Pillar 3, with Pillar 2 for the founder story.
- **AI Systems benefits:** Pillar 1, the lead engine.

## Weekly mix (7 posts)
- 4 x Pillar 1 (leads)
- 2 x Pillar 3 (authority)
- 1 x Pillar 2 (trust)

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Weight output toward Pillar 1 and keep construction as the named niche.
**Evidence:** Your existing assets, market knowledge, and prior trades work sit in construction.
**Reason:** A narrow, warm niche converts faster than broad reach. You already speak the language and have proof.
**Point:** Win construction first, then expand to accountants and professional services once the playbook is proven.

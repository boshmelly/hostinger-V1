# goal.md: Targets and Scoreboard

## Primary goal
Position Melly as a trusted AI systems advisor and future fractional AI partner for SMEs. The objective is qualified calls and revenue, not influence.

## 6-month targets
- 5,000+ followers (lead platform: LinkedIn)
- 500 booked calls (note: see flag below)
- 5 qualified inbound opportunities per week
- £6,000+ monthly revenue
- First paid collaborations
- Clear authority in AI systems and process improvement

## Flag on the 500 calls target (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** 500 booked calls in 6 months is likely the wrong number to chase.
**Evidence:** 500 calls over ~26 weeks is ~19 calls a week. At 30 minutes each that is ~10 hours of calls weekly on top of a full-time job and delivery work.
**Reason:** Calls are a cost, not a win. The win is revenue and qualified pipeline. Chasing call volume pulls you into low-value conversations and burns the scarcest resource: your time.
**Point:** Re-anchor on 5 qualified calls a week (~130 in 6 months) with a tight qualifier before booking. Confirm if you meant 50, not 500.

## The only metrics that matter (weekly scoreboard)
| Metric | Target | Why it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Posts shipped | 7+ | Volume drives everything else |
| Profile views / week | rising | Leading signal of authority |
| Qualified inbound | 5 | The actual pipeline |
| Calls booked | 5 | Pipeline becoming revenue |
| Revenue | £6k/mo by month 6 | The scoreboard |

## Vanity metrics to ignore
Likes. Total views. Follower count above 5k. None of these pay you.

## Milestones
- **Month 1:** 30 posts shipped, audit funnel live, first 5 calls.
- **Month 3:** 2,000 followers, 2-3 paid clients, £2-3k/mo.
- **Month 6:** 5,000 followers, £6k/mo, first collaboration, repeatable inbound.

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Track only the weekly scoreboard above. Review every Friday.
**Evidence:** You run on limited hours around a day job.
**Reason:** What gets measured weekly gets done. A short scoreboard forces focus on revenue, not applause.
**Point:** If a number is not on the scoreboard, stop watching it.

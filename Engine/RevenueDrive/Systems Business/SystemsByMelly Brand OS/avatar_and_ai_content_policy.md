# avatar_and_ai_content_policy.md: AI Use and Avatar Rules

How you use AI to make content without losing trust. The brand sells AI judgement, so your own use of it must be credible.

## Core principle
AI speeds up production. It does not replace your face, voice, or judgement. The trust comes from a real operator showing real work.

## Allowed (use freely)
- Scripting and hook drafting (you edit every line).
- Caption and post writing from your bullet points.
- Repurposing one asset into many formats.
- Research and summarising tools.
- Editing, captions, b-roll, thumbnails.
- AI voiceover for faceless screen-recording posts, clearly your own words.

## Use with care
- AI avatars / talking-head clones: only if clearly labelled and only for low-stakes repurposing. Never for the main founder story.
- Fully AI-generated "opinion" posts: do not ship without your edit. Your take must be yours.

## Not allowed (breaks the brand)
- Faking results, clients, or case studies.
- Passing off AI output as a manual demo.
- Generic AI motivational content with no real substance.
- Hiding that a workflow used AI when the point is to teach AI.

## Disclosure stance
When AI does something impressive on screen, name it. Transparency is the product. "I used Claude to build this in 10 minutes" is the hook, not a confession.

## Avatar / on-camera mix
- 90% of content needs no face: screen recordings, demos, voiceover.
- 10% talking head for the founder story and trust. This is where the real you matters most.
- If you use an AI avatar, restrict it to repurposed text posts, never the personal pillar.

## Quality control
Every AI-assisted post passes one test before shipping: would you stand behind this if a client asked you to explain it live? If no, do not post.

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Keep your real face on the 10% trust content and let AI scale the rest.
**Evidence:** You are selling AI judgement to skeptical SME owners.
**Reason:** If the whole brand looks AI-generated, you undercut the trust that closes deals. A real operator who uses AI well is the exact proof your buyers need.
**Point:** Let AI make you faster, not fake.

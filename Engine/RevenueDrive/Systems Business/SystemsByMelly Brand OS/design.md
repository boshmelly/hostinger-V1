# design.md: SystemsByMelly Brand Design

## Purpose
One reference for how the brand looks, sounds, and feels. Use it so every post is recognisable in 0.5 seconds.

## Brand at a glance
- **Name:** SystemsByMelly
- **Content series:** Melly Builds
- **Tagline:** Building Better Systems For Business
- **Positioning line:** I help business owners use AI to fix slow, messy operations.
- **Primary platforms:** LinkedIn (authority + leads), TikTok/Shorts (reach), YouTube (depth).

## Voice
- Operator, not guru. You ran £39M+ programmes. Speak from delivery, not theory.
- Plain English. If a 50-year-old electrician would not get it, rewrite it.
- Show, do not claim. Screen recordings over selfies.
- Honest. Feature failures and dead ends, not just wins.
- No hype, no fake urgency, no "I cracked the code" bait.

## Tone rules (hard)
- Short sentences. Active voice.
- No em-dashes. Use a full stop or a comma.
- No filler words: literally, unlock, revolutionise, game-changer, dive deep.
- No emojis unless replying casually in comments.

## Visual system
- **Look:** clean, premium, calm. White or dark background, one accent colour, no clutter.
- **Screen-first:** the screen recording is the hero. Face is secondary.
- **Captions:** burned-in, large, high contrast. Most viewers watch on mute.
- **Consistency beats polish.** Same fonts, same intro frame, same end card every time.

## Asset checklist (build once, reuse)
- Logo + wordmark (have: Brand Assets folder)
- One accent colour + one neutral
- Caption template (font, size, position)
- Hook frame template
- End card: "Want this for your business? Free AI audit. Link in bio."

## What "on brand" means
A post is on brand if a stranger watches 10 seconds and thinks: *this person clearly knows how real businesses run, and they just showed me something useful.*

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Lock one visual template before posting volume.
**Evidence:** Your goal is 5,000 followers and 5 inbound leads a week in 6 months, which needs daily output.
**Reason:** You cannot ship daily if every post is a fresh design decision. Templates remove that friction and build recognition.
**Point:** Spend day 1-2 building 3 templates, then never redesign again this quarter.

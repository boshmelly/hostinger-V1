# content_operating_system.md: How Content Gets Made

The system that lets you ship 7 posts a week around a full-time job without burning out.

## Operating principle
Volume beats perfection. A consistent useful post beats a rare perfect one. Build a machine, not a mood.

## The weekly rhythm
- **Sunday (90 min): Batch plan.** Pick 7 ideas from first_100_content_ideas.md. Write 7 hooks.
- **Two weeknights (60 min each): Record.** Screen-record 3-4 demos per session. No editing yet.
- **One night (45 min): Caption + schedule.** Write captions, add CTA, schedule the week.
- **Daily (10 min): Engage.** Reply to every comment within 2 hours. Comment on 5 target accounts.

Total: roughly 5 hours a week. Protect it like a client meeting.

## The content formula (every post)
1. **Hook (first 3 seconds):** a problem, a number, or a bold claim.
2. **Show:** the screen, the workflow, the before/after.
3. **Lesson:** one takeaway the viewer can use.
4. **CTA:** soft on most posts, hard on 2 a week (free AI audit).

## Hook bank (steal these)
- "Most [electricians] lose £X a month to this one admin task."
- "I automated [task] for a [trade] in 12 minutes. Here is how."
- "Stop paying for [tool]. Here is the free version that does the same."
- "This is the AI mistake that costs small businesses the most."
- "I ran £39M programmes. This is the bottleneck I see in every small business."

## Content mix (matches brand)
- 50% screen recordings
- 25% demonstrations
- 15% commentary
- 10% talking head

## Repurposing (1 idea, 4 posts)
One screen recording becomes: a LinkedIn video, a TikTok/Short, a text breakdown post, a carousel. Never make one asset do one job.

## Quality bar (the only 3 checks)
- Does the hook stop the scroll?
- Did I show something useful?
- Is there a clear next step?
If yes to all three, ship it. Do not polish further.

## Tools
- Recording: built-in screen recorder or Loom.
- Captions: auto-caption tool, then fix names.
- Scheduling: one scheduler for all platforms.

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Batch recording on fixed nights, never record ad hoc.
**Evidence:** Daily decision-making is the main reason solo creators quit.
**Reason:** Batching removes the daily "what do I post" tax and protects output when motivation dips.
**Point:** Treat the Sunday plan and two record nights as non-negotiable calendar blocks.

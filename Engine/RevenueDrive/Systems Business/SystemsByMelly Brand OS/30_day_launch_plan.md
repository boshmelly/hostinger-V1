# 30_day_launch_plan.md: First 30 Days

Goal for the month: 30 posts shipped, audit funnel live, first 5 qualified calls. Built to run around a full-time job.

## Week 1: Foundation (build once)
- **Day 1:** Lock visual templates (hook frame, caption style, end card). Update LinkedIn bio and headline.
- **Day 2:** Build the free AI audit: form + 1-page report template + booking page with qualifier.
- **Day 3:** Write 30 hooks from first_100_content_ideas.md. Pick the week's 7.
- **Day 4:** Record 4 screen-recording demos (construction angle).
- **Day 5:** Caption and schedule posts 1-7. Publish post 1.
- **Day 6-7:** Engage. Reply to all comments. Comment on 5 target accounts daily.

## Week 2: Volume (find the rhythm)
- Ship 7 posts (4 Pillar 1, 2 Pillar 3, 1 Pillar 2).
- Start outbound: 5 personalised DMs/day to trades owners. No pitch.
- Soft CTA on 5 posts, hard audit CTA on 2.
- Track: profile views, DMs, audit form starts.

## Week 3: Convert (turn views into talks)
- Ship 7 posts. Double down on whichever post type got most saves/comments in weeks 1-2.
- Push the free audit harder. Offer it directly to engaged commenters via DM.
- Book first calls. Use the qualifier. Aim for 3-5 booked.
- Run your first calls: diagnose one bottleneck, show one fix.

## Week 4: Close and review
- Ship 7 posts. Repurpose your best 2 into carousels and Shorts.
- Convert at least 1 call into a Phase 2 paid project.
- **Friday review:** score the month on the goal.md scoreboard. Kill what did not work. Keep what did.
- Plan month 2 from the data, not guesses.

## Daily non-negotiables (10-15 min)
- Reply to every comment.
- 5 substantive comments on target accounts.
- 5 outbound DMs.

## What to ignore this month
Follower count. Viral chasing. Editing perfection. Building more lead magnets. New platforms. Focus is the strategy.

## Success check at day 30
- 30 posts live
- Audit funnel working end to end
- 5+ qualified calls held
- 1 paid project or a clear hot lead

## Recommendation (P-E-R-P)
**Point:** Do not add anything new until week 4 review.
**Evidence:** New solo brands fail from scattering, not from a weak plan.
**Reason:** One niche, one funnel, one platform, 30 days gives you clean data to decide what scales.
**Point:** Constraints now buy you leverage later.

## Executive Summary

**Arete opportunity lever: Two converging niches create deal flow + operational efficiency compound.**

1. **Property Management (12k+ UK agencies, 80k+ admin staff, 35% churn).** These agencies sit atop property portfolios and building supply chains. RevenueDrive can systemize their operations (tenant comms, maintenance scheduling, rent collection), converting them into inbound lead sources for Arete's block acquisition strategy. Each PM manages 50-300 units and knows upcoming vacancies, fire-door compliance issues, relocation candidates. Bundle offer: RevenueDrive software (£800-2000/mo) + quarterly block acquisition strategy. Expected outcome: 2-3 qualified block deals per quarter from PM client base.

2. **Relocation Agencies (6k+ UK, 8/10 staff churn = constant hiring pain).** These agencies place corporate/contractor/support-worker clients into short-lets and rentals. Arete Stays supplies guest inventory; RevenueDrive supplies back-office automation (client comms, property search workflows, quote generation). Combined play: "We place your clients. We automate your back-office. We source your property supply." Expected outcome: 15-25 properties flowing through Arete Stays per month per agency partner, plus 2-3 renovation/block leads quarterly.

**Ola's direct moat:** BRRR strategy, block management, fire-door certs, Airbnb specialist (contractor, support worker, engineer, corporate niches). Property + relocation ecosystem creates 3-4 month lead time to first block deal, but then recurring revenue (management fees) + relocation volume (Arete Stays commission).

## The Arete Opportunity Ranking

### Primary: Property Management + Relocation Agencies (Converging Leverage)

**Property Management (Tier 1 Priority):** 12k+ UK agencies. Each manages 50-300 units. Revenue model: recurring management fees (8-12% of rent). Deal sourcing: vacant buildings, renovation pipelines, compliance issues (fire doors, electrics), relocation candidates. RevenueDrive positioning: "Automate tenant comms, maintenance scheduling, rent collection. Let me handle your operations so you can focus on sourcing." Bundle: RevenueDrive software (£800-2000/mo) + Ola quarterly strategy calls (block acquisition, renovation opportunity). Expected: 2-3 qualified block deals per PM client per year. Implementation: 3 PMs onboarded in 30 days → 2-3 block deals extracted by week 16.

**Relocation Agencies (Tier 1 Priority):** 6k+ UK. High turnover (8/10 staff churn = constant hiring pain). Relocation demand from corporate/contractor/support workers. Joint-venture model: "We place your clients (Arete Stays property supply). We automate your back-office (RevenueDrive software, £700-1500/mo). We source property (Arete Brokerage). One ecosystem." Expected: 15-25 properties flowing to Arete Stays per month per agency. Plus 2-3 renovation/block acquisition leads quarterly. Implementation: 2-3 agencies signed in 30 days → 30+ properties/mo by week 12.

### Secondary: Surveyors, Highway/Utilities/Construction (Relationship-Based)

**Building Surveyors:** Lower-velocity deal flow but high-value signal. Surveyors conduct pre-renovation assessments and know renovation pipelines. Slow relationship-building (3-6 month lead time), but sticky partnership model. Deprioritize until PMs/relocation volume scales.

**Highway/Utilities/Construction:** Secondary accommodation and relocation demand (crew housing for multi-year projects). Lower priority, as RevenueDrive is primary play for this cohort.

## Next 90 Days: Arete Brokerage Roadmap

### Weeks 1-4: Property Management + Relocation Outreach

**Focus:** Build ICP, outreach list, bundle positioning.

- **Property Management ICP:** 12k+ UK agencies. Target: independently owned (50-300 units), turnover pain (hiring admin roles), visible spend (software subscriptions). Decision maker: Owner, Regional Director, Operations Manager.
- **Relocation Agencies ICP:** 6k+ UK. Target: 10-100 placement/mo (volume signal), visible staff churn (hiring for ops), existing tech stack (CRM, scheduling). Decision maker: Owner, Operations Director, Regional Manager.
- **Bundle positioning:** "We automate your back-office AND we source your properties. One partnership, two revenue streams (RevenueDrive software + Arete Stays commissions + block acquisition deals)."
- **Outreach sequence:** LinkedIn + email (Ola introduction: property background, BRRR track record) + physical letter (handwritten). Message: "Let's solve your operational chaos and find your next deal together."
- **Outreach list:** 30 property management agencies + 20 relocation agencies (priority markets: London, Manchester, Birmingham).

**Goal:** 20-30 discovery calls by week 4. 5-10 warm prospects.

### Weeks 5-8: PM Partnership + Relocation Joint-Venture

**Focus:** Move warm prospects to pilots. Lock in first partnerships.

- **Property Management:** 2-3 pilot partnerships signed. Deliver RevenueDrive stack (tenant comms AI, maintenance scheduling, rent collection workflows). During week 6-7, start quarterly strategy calls (block acquisition focus). Extract deal pipeline signals (vacant buildings, renovation candidates, relocation needs).
- **Relocation Agencies:** 1-2 joint-venture partnerships signed. Launch RevenueDrive software rollout. Begin referring placements to Arete Stays. Set up commission split (15-20% of placement value typical). Measure: track placements, property flow, lead quality.

**Goal:** 2 PM partnerships signed + 1 relocation joint-venture signed. First block deal sourced from PM client.

### Weeks 9-12: Scale + First Deal Closure

**Focus:** Expand partnership base. Close first block acquisition from PM client.

- **Property Management:** Expand to 3-5 PM partnerships. Launch batch 2 outreach (10 more agencies). First block deal from PM #1 should be in pipeline (due diligence, funding). Revenue: RevenueDrive software (£800-2000/mo) + block acquisition fee (£2-5k on close).

**Goal:** 5-10 PM + relocation partnerships live. 30+ properties flowing to Arete Stays/mo. 1 block deal closed or near-close.

**MRR target by month 6:** £18-52k (PM software + relocation software + Arete Stays commissions + block fee upside). Plus 2-3 block acquisition deals in portfolio (long-term asset income).

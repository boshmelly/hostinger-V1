## Revenue Drive — ICP, Client Journey, and Growth Strategy (Updated 2026-05-26)

### The Business Model
Revenue Drive helps independent tradespeople and small service businesses get found on Google, generate more inbound calls, and build a full AI-powered inbound system. The end goal is to operate as a Fractional AI Partner for larger firms who can afford a full-stack engagement.

Electricians are the entry vertical. Not the destination. They are the case study factory. Every client closed, every profile fixed, every increase in inbound calls becomes the proof used to move up to higher-value phases.

Note: Electricians sit in the "Avoid" tier for niche ranking (low search value per lead). Use Phase 1 to build proof of concept, then pivot to Phase 2+ demographics.

### The Upsell Ladder

Tier 1 — Google Visibility (entry product): Google Business Profile optimisation + local SEO. Monthly. Gets them ranking, gets them calls.

Tier 2 — Website: One-time build plus optional maintenance. Unlocked once Tier 1 proves inbound results.

Tier 3 — AI Receptionist: Call handling, email response, WhatsApp and chatbot. High margin. Solves the "missing calls at 9pm" problem. This is where revenue per client scales.

Tier 4 — Fractional AI Partner: Full inbound system management. Visibility, web, comms, lead qualification, reporting. Retained at significantly higher price point.

### Client Journey Phases

Phase 1 (Now — 0 to 12 months): Independent tradespeople. Electricians, roofers, HVAC, kitchen fitters. 1 to 5 staff. UK. Weak or no Google presence. Good word of mouth but invisible online. Paying £300 to £600/month.

Phase 2 (6 to 18 months): Small business owners in service trades. 5 to 20 staff. Needs website, visibility, and AI layer to stop missing leads. Paying £800 to £1,500/month.

Phase 3 (18 to 36 months): Professional services firms. Mortgage brokers, IFAs, senior care operators, legal firms. 10 to 50 staff. Needs a full inbound revenue engine run by a retained partner. Paying £2,000 to £5,000/month.

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Service: Local SEO + Google visibility + AI receptionist + website for independent UK tradespeople and small service businesses.

Target industry: Independent electricians (Phase 1). Expands to homeowner-adjacent trades (roofers, HVAC, kitchen fitters, window installers) then to small business owners demographic (Phase 2).

Target location: UK. Suburban and urban areas with active property development, landlords, and letting agents.

Ideal business size: 1 to 5 operatives. Owner-operator or small team. Owner still takes calls and quotes jobs.

Signs they NEED the service:
- Not on page 1 of Google for their city + trade
- Google Business profile incomplete (no photos, no service area, no posts)
- Website thin, outdated, or does not exist
- Fewer than 20 Google reviews, or reviews not responded to
- A weaker competitor ranks above them
- Relies entirely on word of mouth with no digital inbound channel

Signs they can AFFORD the service:
- Trading 2+ years with established cash flow
- 10+ Google reviews at 4 stars or above
- Portfolio includes rewires, consumer unit upgrades, or commercial work (not just small call-outs)
- Has a website (even basic) — signals prior investment in presence
- Active on any social platform

Disqualifiers:
- Already ranking on page 1 for their area
- Diary full with the right type of work (property managers, project managers, commercial clients) and no desire to grow
- Has in-house marketing or active agency
- Operates purely on contract with no need for consumer inbound
- Only wants project-based or minor jobs (PAT testing, small call-outs) with no interest in scaling
- Wants cheap one-off work only

### Demographics Expansion Strategy (Later Phase)
Rather than single-vertical targeting, expand by demographic group:
- Homeowners demographic: covers roofers, HVAC, kitchen renovation, solar, window installation (C-level niches but high volume at demographic level)
- Small business owners demographic: commercial insurance, marketing, banking, software (B-level, higher margin)
- Professional services (A-level): wealth management, mortgage, senior care, legal

## Market Intelligence

**What this run reveals:**

1. **London hybrid is the norm for senior enterprise CS roles.** AI/SaaS companies (Intercom/Fin, Ultralytics) that hire enterprise CSMs at £50k+ levels are pulling people in for 2-3 days. This is the biggest filter removing otherwise qualifying roles.

2. **Fully remote enterprise CS roles at senior level are rare in UK/EMEA.** Most fully remote CS roles that exist are either: (a) US-based with no sponsorship, (b) SMB/mid-market scale not enterprise, or (c) operational/admin roles not commercial account ownership.

3. **RevOps listings that are fully remote require technical admin skills.** The "remote-friendly RevOps" market is skewed toward CRM administrators and automation engineers, not RevOps strategists.

4. **Marketing Automation Manager roles did not surface in this run.** UK/EMEA Indeed searches returned zero results. This vertical needs a different search strategy — try LinkedIn and specific company career pages (HubSpot, Marketo ecosystem agencies, GoHighLevel partners).

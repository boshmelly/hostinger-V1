## The Authority Reframe (use this version)

- Coordinated £38M+ of TfL infrastructure projects alongside Tier 1/3 contractors (Ringways, Taylor Woodrow, Marlborough, Cadent Gas, Thames Water)
- Started as a man on site (temporary traffic management operative) — ground-level credibility, not theory
- Introduced a single process change (amendment fees) that cut admin by 17% and increased revenue by 25% — this is the systems story
- Managed contractor chaos at scale: procurement, consents, risk, regulatory compliance, multi-stakeholder coordination
- Crossed into DevOps, data governance (CDMP + PMP), and property operations — unusually broad operator profile

## Social Content Angles (electricians / trades audience)

- "I started on the tools. Here's what nine years on infrastructure taught me about AI."
- "I coordinated Cadent Gas and Thames Water simultaneously. The admin alone was killing efficiency. Now I automate it."
- "One process change. Admin down 17%. Revenue up 25%. That's not AI — that's a system. AI makes it faster."

- "Nine years in highways. Then I asked — what's the most leveraged version of what I know?"

## What to Avoid

- Passive CV language: "aided in", "contributed to", "coordinated project tasks as directed"
- Listing job titles or council names without a story attached
- Regulatory acronyms (NRSWA, DCO, S106) without explaining the human cost/complexity

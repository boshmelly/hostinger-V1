## Non-negotiable evidence standards

These apply to every piece of intelligence you produce:

- **No opinion without 3+ independent sources** cross-referenced per claim or industry.
- **Quotes, not summaries.** When citing community pain (Reddit, forums, Facebook groups, LinkedIn), extract verbatim quotes with the source.
- **Numbers cited, not guessed.** Hiring volumes from named job boards, business counts from official statistics or trade bodies, pricing from published rate cards or documented deals.
- **Willingness to pay is justified**, never assumed. Show what they already spend (salaries, software, subcontractors) that proves the budget exists.
- Flag confidence levels honestly. A weak signal labelled weak is useful. A weak signal dressed up as strong is poison.

## Flagship mission: Market Opportunity Intelligence

When Ola asks for the market scan (or any slice of it), run this protocol. The goal: identify UK/Ireland industries where AI operations automation creates outsized value before the market saturates. High operational pain, spending power, hiring demand, low competition. Not a list, a ranked, evidence-backed investigation.

### The 19 industries

- **Property & Letting:** Property Management, Letting Agencies, Relocation Agencies, Building Surveyors, Property Maintenance
- **Trades & Engineering:** Electrical Contractors ★, M&E Contractors, Commercial Cleaning, Security, Engineering Consultancies
- **Infrastructure & Compliance:** Highway Contractors ★, Utilities Contractors ★, Infrastructure Consultants ★, Construction Project Managers ★, Facilities Management ★
- **Insurance & Compliance:** Insurance Restoration, Loss Adjusters, Fire Safety Consultants, Compliance Consultants

★ = high alignment with Ola's TfL/infrastructure background.

### Research protocol, per industry

1. **Size & financials.** UK/Ireland business count, typical employee range, revenue, margins, growth.
2. **Reddit pain signals.** Search terms: "admin", "can't keep up", "scheduling", "phone never stops", "compliance", "spreadsheet", "burnout". Extract recurring complaints as verbatim quotes.
3. **Industry press.** Trade associations, LinkedIn discussions, Facebook groups, blogs, trade news.
4. **Hiring volume.** Indeed, Reed, TotalJobs, LinkedIn. Roles: Operations, Admin, Scheduler, Estimator, Project Coordinator, Compliance, Document Controller. High volume = automation signal. Cite counts.
5. **Staff turnover.** Retention rates, recruitment struggles, skills shortages, cost of hiring.
6. **Repetitive processes.** Quote generation, scheduling, RAMS, compliance paperwork, CRM updates, invoice chasing, customer onboarding.
7. **Automation fit.** Map pains to: Claude, n8n/Make, Voice AI, AI receptionist, OCR, email automation, knowledge bases.
8. **Red tape.** GDPR exposure, sector regulation, data privacy, integration complexity. Rate Low/Medium/High.
9. **Willingness to pay.** Can they afford £500 / £1k / £2.5k / £5k per month? Justify with evidence of existing spend.
10. **Buyer identification.** Who signs the cheque: Owner, MD, Ops Director, Facilities Manager.

### Scoring

Score each industry 1 to 10 on ten dimensions: Operational Pain, Profit Margin, Hiring Demand, Staff Turnover, Admin Burden, AI Readiness, Competition (lower competition = higher score), Decision Maker Accessibility, Sales Cycle (shorter = higher), Ease of Implementation.

- **Opportunity Score (1 to 10):** weighted composite of the ten dimensions. State your weights.
- **Blue Ocean Score (1 to 10):** Ola's specific competitive advantage in the sector. Factor: low AI agency competition, high operational complexity, trust requirements, recurring revenue potential, and Ola's TfL/infrastructure/property/Salesforce moat.

Every score justified, evidence cited inline or footnoted.

Baked-in evidence throughout: Reddit quotes verbatim, job board volumes cited, willingness to pay justified.

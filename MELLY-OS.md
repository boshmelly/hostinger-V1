## Interdependencies — the branches are NOT silos

These flows are what make it a *system* rather than a list. Never optimise one branch in isolation.

- **Personal Growth → energy → ALL** — depleted root, degraded everything.
- **Family → purpose → Businesses** — the why you push.
- **Career & History → credibility → Businesses** — the proof that closes sales.
- **Content Creation → visibility → ALL businesses** — inbound and authority.
- **Businesses → revenue → Family + Personal Growth** — the loop closes; money funds the foundation and the why.
- **AreteStays ⇄ Arete Brokerage** — shared property pipeline and deal flow.

## How the OS thinks (read order for any session)

1. **Start at YOU** — judge the task against your top-level goals: freedom, energy, meaningful work, family balance, peace.
2. **Load the global AI layer** — skills, prompts, memory are available automatically.
3. **Identify the active branch** — which domain is this task in?
4. **Check interdependencies** — what does it pull from or feed into other branches?
5. **Route to the right venture / agent** — execute toward completion.
6. **Write learnings back to memory** — the brain compounds.
